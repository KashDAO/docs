**HTTP status:** 404 · **Title:** "Redemptions not enabled"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/redemptions` was called while self-service redemption is not enabled on the platform. Resolved markets are then paid out automatically, without any claim from you.

## Why it happens

- Self-service redemption has not been enabled in this environment. Markets are then paid out automatically at resolution.
- The capability was switched off platform-wide.

This is distinct from [`ROUTE_DISABLED`](/developer-docs/api-errors/ROUTE_DISABLED), a temporary `503` that pauses the endpoint. `REDEMPTIONS_NOT_ENABLED` means the capability is not enabled at all, so retrying will not help.

## How to fix

- Nothing to do as an integrator. Winnings are still paid out automatically once the market resolves; check `GET /v1/portfolio` for the settled balance.
- If you believe self-service redemption should be enabled for your environment, contact Kash support.
