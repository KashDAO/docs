**HTTP status:** 403 · **Title:** "Not the group owner"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/join` was called to enter a group the caller does not own, or to create an individual entry on someone else's behalf.

## Why it happens

- **Group entry:** only a member with the `owner` role may enter the group into a competition.
- **Individual entry:** the entrant must be the caller.

## Why entering and funding differ

Entering is an act of the group; funding is an act of a member. Any member may pay into an existing entry (see [`COMPETITION_NOT_ENTRY_MEMBER`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_NOT_ENTRY_MEMBER)), but only the owner may commit the group to a competition, because the entry takes one of the competition's limited slots and binds the whole group.

## How to fix

- Ask a group owner to create the entry, then contribute to it.
- For an individual entry, call as the entrant.
