**HTTP status:** 503 · **Title:** "Trading busy" · **Retry-After:** computed per response

## When it fires

`POST /v1/trades` (or `POST /v1/trades/{id}/confirm`) while Kash is working through a trading backlog: the
oldest queued trade work has waited longer than your key's tier tolerates. Kash refuses new trades at the door rather than
accepting them and letting them expire in the queue.

**Nothing was placed.** The request was refused before anything was written, so retrying it, with the same
`Idempotency-Key` or `clientRequestId`, is a fresh attempt and never a duplicate. This is system-wide and transient;
it says nothing about your account.

Higher tiers tolerate a longer backlog before being refused, so `enterprise` and `mm` keys keep trading through a
short surge that already turns `free` and `developer` keys away.

## Response body

In addition to the standard fields, the problem body carries a top-level `queueAgeSeconds` member: the age of the oldest queued trade work when you were refused.

## How to fix

- **Honour `Retry-After`.** The TypeScript SDK retries 503 responses automatically and honours `Retry-After`.
- Don't treat it as an outage of your integration: read endpoints are unaffected, and trades
  already accepted still execute.
