**HTTP status:** 403 · **Title:** "Not a member of this entry"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/contribute` was called by someone who does not belong to the entry they tried to contribute to.

## Why it happens

- For a **group** entry, the caller is not a member of that group.
- For an **individual** entry, the caller is not the entrant.

The contributing member is always the authenticated caller; it is never read from the request body, so this cannot be worked around by naming a different user.

## Why it is refused rather than accepted

Contributions determine each member's share of a group's reward allocation, so only the entry's own members may contribute to it.

## How to fix

- Join the group before contributing to its entry.
- Check that the `entrantId` in the body is the entry you meant. Contributing to another group's entry by mistake produces this error.
