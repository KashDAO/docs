**HTTP status:** 404 · **Title:** "No refundable contribution"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/refund` on a **cancelled** competition where you have no outstanding refundable contribution.

## Why it happens

Two situations produce it, deliberately with the same answer:

1. **You never contributed** to any entry in this competition.
2. **You have already been refunded.** The refundable amount is your contribution minus refunds already paid to you, so once you are paid there is nothing left.

The second case is what makes the endpoint safe to retry: a repeat call after a successful refund finds nothing to do and returns this code, rather than paying you twice. It is a final state more than an error.

It is scoped to **you**. In a pooled group entry each member is refunded their own contribution independently; another member still being owed money does not reopen your settled refund.

## How to fix

- If you expected a refund, check the contribution was made by the account whose API key you are using. Contributions are attributed to the authenticated caller.
- If you already received the refund, no action is needed.

## Related codes

- [`COMPETITION_NOT_CANCELLED`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_NOT_CANCELLED) — the competition is not cancelled, so nothing is refundable yet
- [`COMPETITION_REFUND_FAILED`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_REFUND_FAILED) — a refund was found but could not be recorded
