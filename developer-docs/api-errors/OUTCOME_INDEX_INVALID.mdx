**HTTP status:** 400 · **Title:** "Outcome index invalid"

## When it fires

`outcomeIndex` is negative, or greater than or equal to the market's number of outcomes. Returned by `POST /v1/trades`, `GET /v1/markets/{id}/quote` and `POST /v1/redemptions`.

## Why it happens

- You hard-coded `outcomeIndex: 1` assuming a binary market and pointed it at a market with a different shape.
- Off by one: the field is **zero-indexed**. The first outcome is `0`, not `1`.
- The index was computed from a label without a bounds check.

## How to fix

- Fetch the market with `GET /v1/markets/{id}` and check `outcomes.length`. The valid range is `0` to `length - 1`.
- Map outcome labels to indices once when you fetch the market, then use the index throughout your code.
