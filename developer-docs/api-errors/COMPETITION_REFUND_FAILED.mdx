**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Refund could not be recorded"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/refund` found an outstanding amount for you, but recording the refund was then refused.

## Why it happens

The refund amount is checked **twice, independently**: once when working out what you are owed, and again when the refund is recorded, which caps it at your own recorded contribution. This code means the two checks disagreed.

That should not happen, and it is reported rather than retried on purpose. The second check is the authority (it guarantees a member of a group entry cannot be refunded more than they contributed), so when the two disagree the safe action is to pay nothing and say so.

The realistic cause is a concurrent refund for the same contribution landing between the two checks.

## How to fix

- **Retry once.** If a concurrent refund completed, the retry finds nothing outstanding and returns [`COMPETITION_NOTHING_TO_REFUND`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_NOTHING_TO_REFUND), which means you have been refunded.
- If it persists, stop retrying and contact Kash support with the `requestId`. The `detail` includes the amounts that were refused.
