**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Competition prize auto-distributed"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/claim` was called for a competition whose rewards are **distributed automatically** (settlement mode `offchain_airdrop` or `none`). Only `onchain_escrow` and `sponsored` competitions are claimed through this endpoint.

## Why it happens

The competition settles by automatic distribution, so there is nothing to claim: rewards arrive without a caller-initiated claim.

This is a state conflict on the competition, not an authorization failure about you (that would be `403` [`COMPETITION_PRIZE_NOT_CLAIMABLE`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_PRIZE_NOT_CLAIMABLE)), so it is a permanent `409` and retrying will not help.

## How to fix

- Nothing to do on the claim endpoint. Any reward allocated to you is distributed automatically; check your balance and history instead of claiming.
