**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Already in another entry"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/contribute` was called by someone who is already funding a **different** entry in the same competition.

## Why it happens

Each member funds at most one entry per competition. The contribution is refused when the caller has already contributed to another entry in that competition.

## Why it is refused rather than accepted

Contributions decide the prize split, and standings are attributed per entry. A member funding two entries in one competition would hold a share of two competing prize pools, and their trading would count for both, so the competition could not be scored fairly.

## How to fix

- Contribute to the entry you already belong to. `GET /v1/competitions/{competitionId}` lists your entry.
- If you meant to move to a different entry, leave the first one before funding another. A contribution already made cannot be transferred between entries.

## Related codes

- [`COMPETITION_NOT_ENTRY_MEMBER`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_NOT_ENTRY_MEMBER) — the caller does not belong to the entry at all
- [`COMPETITION_ENTRY_NOT_FUNDABLE`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_ENTRY_NOT_FUNDABLE) — the entry or competition is not accepting money
