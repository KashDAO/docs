**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Already in another entry"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/contribute` was called by someone who already contributes to a **different** entry in the same competition.

## Why it happens

Each member contributes to at most one entry per competition. The contribution is refused when the caller has already contributed to another entry in that competition.

## Why it is refused rather than accepted

Standings are attributed per entry, and contributions determine each member's share of a group's reward allocation. A member contributing to two entries in one competition would have their trading count for both, so the standings could not be calculated fairly.

## How to fix

- Contribute to the entry you already belong to. `GET /v1/competitions/{competitionId}` lists your entry.
- If you meant to move to a different entry, leave the first one before contributing to another. A contribution already made cannot be transferred between entries.

## Related codes

- [`COMPETITION_NOT_ENTRY_MEMBER`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_NOT_ENTRY_MEMBER) — the caller does not belong to the entry at all
- [`COMPETITION_ENTRY_NOT_FUNDABLE`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_ENTRY_NOT_FUNDABLE) — the entry or competition is not accepting contributions
