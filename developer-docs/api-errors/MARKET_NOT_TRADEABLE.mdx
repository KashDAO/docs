**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Market not tradeable"

## When it fires

The market exists but does not accept trades right now. The reason is in `detail`.

- `POST /v1/trades` and `POST /v1/trades/{id}/confirm` return it for every reason below, on both API versions.
- `GET /v1/markets/{id}/quote` returns it for a market cancelled on-chain, and for a Solana market whose on-chain state is no longer active (for example resolved). A quote is a read, so a market past its freeze deadline can still be quoted, with `market.tradeable: false` in the response.
- `GET /v1/markets/{id}/quote` also returns it for a market that has no on-chain address yet and so cannot be quoted at all.
- **Pinning `2026-04-29` hides Solana markets.** On that pinned version, a Solana market's detail and quote return [`CHAIN_NOT_SUPPORTED`](/developer-docs/api-errors/CHAIN_NOT_SUPPORTED) (400) whatever its state, which says nothing about whether it is tradeable.

## Why it happens

The reasons, in the order they are checked. On `2026-08-19`, each one is published on the market itself as `notTradeableReason`:

| `notTradeableReason` | Meaning                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `resolved`           | The outcome is final. Winning positions are redeemable.                                     |
| `cancelled`          | The market was cancelled on-chain. Positions are refundable through `POST /v1/redemptions`. |
| `inactive`           | The market's status is not one that trades (for example, not yet seeded).                   |
| `frozen`             | The market's freeze deadline (`freezeAt`) has passed. Trading has closed.                   |

`status` alone does not show `cancelled` or `frozen`: it stays `ACTIVE` for both. The market may also have changed between your quote and your trade.

## How to fix

- Read `tradeable` on the market (`GET /v1/markets/{id}`, `GET /v1/markets`, or the `market` object in a quote) before submitting. It is computed by the same rule that returns this error, so `tradeable: false` means a trade would be refused.
- `resolved`, `cancelled` and `frozen` are final, so retrying the same trade will not succeed. Choose another market, or redeem your position when the reason is `resolved` or `cancelled`.
