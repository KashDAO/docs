**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Competition entry not fundable"

## When it fires

The competition or entry is not accepting money right now. Returned by `POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/contribute`, and by `/join` and `/sponsor` when the competition itself is not open for funding.

## Why it happens

- The competition does not collect entry contributions. Only `onchain_escrow` competitions do; `sponsored`, `offchain_airdrop` and `none` competitions have nothing to fund.
- The competition is outside its funding window: before submissions open, or at or after `join_deadline`.
- The competition is no longer `published` or `registration` (it has started, been cancelled or settled).
- The entry no longer accepts contributions because it was withdrawn, rejected, disqualified or refunded. Entries in `funding` or `joined` accept contributions; nothing else does.

A fully funded entry still accepts contributions: a member may top up a `joined` entry, which increases their share of any prize. The competition's window, not the funding threshold, closes the door.

## How to fix

- Check the competition's state and `join_deadline` with `GET /v1/competitions/{competitionId}` before contributing.
- If the entry was refunded or withdrawn, create a new entry and fund that instead.

This is a state conflict, not an authorization failure. Retrying will not help until the state changes.
