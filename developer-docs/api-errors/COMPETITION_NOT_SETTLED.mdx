**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Competition not settled"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/claim` was called for a competition that has not reached a claimable state. Rewards can only be claimed once the final standings are settled.

## Why it happens

- The competition is still `published`, `registration`, `running` or `scoring_frozen`, so the final standings are not settled yet.
- The competition was `cancelled`, so there is no reward to claim. Contributors are refunded through `POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/refund`.

## How to fix

- Wait until the competition is `settled` (or `paid_out`).
- Check the competition's state with `GET /v1/competitions/{competitionId}` before claiming.
