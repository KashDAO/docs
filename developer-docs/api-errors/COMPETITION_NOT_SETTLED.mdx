**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Competition not settled"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/claim` was called for a competition that has not reached a claimable state. Prizes can only be claimed once winners have been declared.

## Why it happens

- The competition is still `published`, `registration`, `running` or `scoring_frozen`, so winners have not been declared yet.
- The competition was `cancelled`, so there is no prize to claim. Paid entrants are refunded through `POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/refund`.

## How to fix

- Wait until the competition is `settled` (or `paid_out`). Winners are declared automatically once scoring is frozen and the prize pot is funded.
- Check the competition's state with `GET /v1/competitions/{competitionId}` before claiming.
