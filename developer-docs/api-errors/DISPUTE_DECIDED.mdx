**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Dispute already decided"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/markets/{id}/disputes` was called for a market whose disputes Kash has already decided. When a proposal is disputed, Kash decides the final outcome: the proposed one, a different one, or cancelling the market. That decision is final, is settled immediately and cannot itself be disputed, so every later filing on the market is refused.

## Why it happens

- Another user disputed the proposal, and the market was decided before your filing arrived.

## How to fix

- There is nothing to retry: the decision is final, and a retry is refused the same way.
- Follow the settlement through `GET /v1/markets/{id}`. If you filed a dispute before the decision, its outcome (`status`, `reviewedAt`, `reviewNote`) is on `GET /v1/markets/{id}/disputes`.
