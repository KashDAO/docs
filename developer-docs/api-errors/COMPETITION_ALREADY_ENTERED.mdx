**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Already entered this competition"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/join` was called for a group (or user) that already has an entry in this competition.

## Why it happens

Entries are unique per competition: one per group, one per user. The existing entry may still be in `funding` (not yet fully paid), which is exactly when a caller is most likely to try joining again.

## Why a second entry is refused rather than created

The entry contribution is shared by the group. A second entry would split the group's contributions across two entries, and neither might reach the required amount, so the group would pay twice and compete in neither.

## How to fix

- Fund the **existing** entry: `POST /v1/competitions/{competitionId}/contribute` with that entry's `entrantId`.
- A withdrawn or rejected entry does not block a new one; any other state does.

## Related codes

- [`COMPETITION_ENTRY_NOT_FUNDABLE`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_ENTRY_NOT_FUNDABLE) — the entry exists but is not accepting money
- [`COMPETITION_NOT_GROUP_OWNER`](/developer-docs/api-errors/COMPETITION_NOT_GROUP_OWNER) — the caller may not create the entry
