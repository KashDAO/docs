**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Insufficient balance"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/redemptions` was called for an outcome your account holds **none** of. Kash read the account's on-chain balance of that outcome token and it was zero, so there is nothing to redeem.

`POST /v1/trades` does not return this code. A trade's USDC balance is checked after the trade is accepted; a trade the account cannot fund ends as `failed` or `rejected`, visible on `GET /v1/trades/{id}` and in trade webhooks.

## Why it happens

- The tokens were already redeemed or moved.
- The account never held that outcome (for example, the wrong `outcomeIndex`).
- The request was made with a key belonging to a different account from the one holding the position.

## How to fix

- Check what the account holds with `GET /v1/portfolio/positions`, and redeem an outcome that appears there.
- Confirm the `marketId` and `outcomeIndex`.
- If you expected the position to have been redeemed already, check `GET /v1/portfolio`.
