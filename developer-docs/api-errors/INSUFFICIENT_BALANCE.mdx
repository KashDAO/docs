**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Insufficient balance"

## When it fires

It fires on two routes.

**`POST /v1/redemptions`** was called for an outcome your account holds **none** of. The API read the account's on-chain balance of that outcome token and it was zero, so there is nothing to redeem.

**`POST /v1/partner/accounts/{accountId}/withdrawals`** (and its quote) asked for more than the account can withdraw: more than its `availableAtomic` from `GET /v1/balance` (its on-chain USDC less what its pending withdrawals hold), or, to Arc, more than `availableAtomic` less the most the network fee can cost, since that fee is paid on top of the amount. The `detail` gives the figures. Nothing was recorded. Withdraw at most the quote's `maxAmountAtomic`, or wait for pending withdrawals to finish. See [Withdrawals](/developer-docs/partner-integration/withdrawals).

`POST /v1/trades` does not return this code. A trade's USDC balance is checked after the trade is accepted; a trade the account cannot fund ends as `failed` or `rejected`, visible on `GET /v1/trades/{id}` and in trade webhooks.

The rest of this page is about redemptions.

## Why it happens

- The tokens were already redeemed or moved.
- The account never held that outcome (for example, the wrong `outcomeIndex`).
- The request was made with a key belonging to a different account from the one holding the position.

## How to fix

- Check what the account holds with `GET /v1/portfolio/positions`, and redeem an outcome that appears there.
- Confirm the `marketId` and `outcomeIndex`.
- If you expected the position to have been redeemed already, check `GET /v1/portfolio`.
