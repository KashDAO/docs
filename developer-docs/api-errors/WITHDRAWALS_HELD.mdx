**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Withdrawals held"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/partner/accounts/{accountId}/withdrawals` was called within 24 hours of a change to the account's destination.

## Why it happens

Withdrawals only ever go to the address you registered for the user. Pausing them for 24 hours after the address changes means a leaked key cannot change the address and take the money out in one go. You also receive an `account.destination_changed` webhook, so an unexpected change can be caught in that window. Nothing was recorded.

## How to fix

- Wait for the time in the `Retry-After` header, then send the withdrawal again with the same `Idempotency-Key`.
- If you did not make the change, suspend the account (`POST /v1/partner/accounts/{accountId}/suspend`) and contact ZeroID support from the info button in the Kash web app → **Feedback**, with the `accountId` and the id of the key to revoke.

## Response headers

- `Retry-After: <seconds>`: how long until the hold ends, 24 hours after the destination change.
