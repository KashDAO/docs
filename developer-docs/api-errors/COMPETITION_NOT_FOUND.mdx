**HTTP status:** 404 · **Title:** "Competition not found"

## When it fires

The `competitionId` in the path does not correspond to any competition. Returned by `GET /v1/competitions/{competitionId}` (and its `entrants` and `leaderboard`), and by the `claim`, `refund` and `sponsor` endpoints.

## Why it happens

- The `competitionId` is a well-formed UUID but names no competition: a typo, the wrong environment, or a draft that was deleted.

## How to fix

- Verify the `competitionId`. List competitions with `GET /v1/competitions`.
