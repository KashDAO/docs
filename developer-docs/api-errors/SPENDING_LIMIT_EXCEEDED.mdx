**HTTP status:** 409 · **Title:** "Spending limit exceeded"

## When it fires

`POST /v1/trades` was called with an `amount` above your API key's **per-trade limit**. The `detail` field states the amount and the limit.

Your key also has a **daily spending limit** (rolling 24 hours, across all trades). That limit is checked after the trade is accepted: a trade that would exceed it is accepted with `202` and then ends as `rejected`, visible on `GET /v1/trades/{id}` and in trade webhooks, rather than returning this code synchronously.

Your key's tier sets both limits, and individual keys can be configured with lower ones. The `detail` field states the limit that applied.

## Why it happens

- A bot computed a larger order than intended (for example a sizing factor was off).
- The key's limit was lowered since you designed your sizing logic.

## How to fix

- For a per-trade overrun, split the order into smaller trades under the limit.
- For daily rejections, wait for the rolling window to clear or move to a higher tier.
- Keep your client's sizing logic aware of your key's limits.

## How to raise the limit

Limits follow your key's tier. See [Rate limits](/developer-docs/rest-api/rate-limits), then:

- **`free` → `developer`**: email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot).
- **`enterprise`** or **`mm`**: [contact sales](mailto:sales@kash.bot).

The limits are not punitive. They bound the damage a leaked key or runaway bot can do, and protect market liquidity. When you outgrow your tier, the limit is the signal to upgrade, not something to work around.
