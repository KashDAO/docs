---
title: 'ORGANIZATION_INACTIVE'
description: 'HTTP 403 — Account inactive'
---

**HTTP status:** 403 · The key's account is inactive.

## When it fires

The API key is valid, not revoked and not expired, but the account it belongs to is `suspended` or `cancelled`.

## Why it happens

- The account was suspended (for example after an abuse report).
- The account was closed.

## How to fix

- Contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot). The key starts working again as soon as the account is active; it does not need to be reissued.

## Related codes

- [`API_KEY_REVOKED`](/developer-docs/api-errors/API_KEY_REVOKED) — the key itself was withdrawn
- [`INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE`](/developer-docs/api-errors/INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE) — also 403, caused by scopes rather than the account
