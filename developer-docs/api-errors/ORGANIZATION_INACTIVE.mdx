**HTTP status:** 403 · **Title:** "Organization inactive"

## When it fires

The API key is valid, not revoked and not expired, but the organization it belongs to is `suspended` or `cancelled`.

## Why it happens

- The organization was suspended (for example after an abuse report or an administrative action).
- The organization's subscription was cancelled.

Organizations in `trial` or `active` status are unaffected.

## How to fix

- Contact Kash support to restore the organization. The key starts working again as soon as the organization is `trial` or `active`; it does not need to be reissued.

## Related codes

- [`API_KEY_REVOKED`](/developer-docs/api-errors/API_KEY_REVOKED) — the key itself was withdrawn
- [`INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE`](/developer-docs/api-errors/INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE) — also 403, caused by scopes rather than the organization
