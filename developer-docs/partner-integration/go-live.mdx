---
title: 'Go-live checklist'
description: 'What to have in place before your partner integration goes to production.'
---

## Approval and keys

- [ ] ZeroID has approved your organisation for production (one-time; ask from the info button in the Kash web app → **Feedback**, or in [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/n9eumqjMPb)).
- [ ] You sent ZeroID your servers' egress addresses for the IP allowlist, each a single address, an IPv4 range of `/16` or narrower, or an IPv6 range of `/48` or narrower (at most 50).
- [ ] You sent ZeroID your production webhook URL.
- [ ] ZeroID issued your `kash_live_` partner key and its webhook secret, and you received them.
- [ ] If you need `GET /v1/account/usage`, you asked ZeroID for the `auth:manage` scope on the key.
- [ ] Keys and webhook secrets live in your secret store on your servers, never in an app, a browser, a log or a repository.
- [ ] Your servers call `https://api.kash.bot/v1`, and no test key is configured in production. A staging key is not known there and is refused with `401 API_KEY_INVALID`.

## Webhooks

- [ ] The production webhook URL on the live key uses `https`, and its signing secret is installed.
- [ ] Your endpoint verifies `X-Kash-Signature` against the raw body and rejects stale timestamps.
- [ ] It deduplicates on the event id, and stores processed ids.
- [ ] It answers `2xx` within 10 seconds and does slow work afterwards.
- [ ] It treats each event as a prompt to read current state, and does not depend on the order events arrive in.
- [ ] It handles every event type, including `account.provisioning_failed`, `withdrawal.failed` and `account.destination_changed`, and alerts your team on a destination change you did not make.

## Accounts and consent

- [ ] You create an account only after the user's own action in your app, and send `consent.acceptedAt` and the `termsVersion` they saw.
- [ ] You send each user's true country, and update it with `PATCH` when it changes.
- [ ] Each user's destination is their own address in your app, on `solana` or `arc`. On Solana it is the user's wallet address, never a token account address.
- [ ] You store the `accountId` against your user, and can find it again with `GET /v1/partner/accounts?externalId=`.

## Money

- [ ] You fund accounts only with USDC on Solana, to the account's `depositAddress`.
- [ ] You show users `availableAtomic` as their balance, and parse every amount as an integer.
- [ ] You quote every withdrawal first and show `receiveAtomic`, `networkFeeAtomic` and `totalDebitAtomic`, and never request more than `maxAmountAtomic`.
- [ ] Every withdrawal carries an `Idempotency-Key` you keep with the request on your side, and every retry reuses it.
- [ ] Every trade carries a `clientRequestId` set to your order id, stored before you send, and every retry of the order reuses it.
- [ ] Each account create uses a new `Idempotency-Key` per onboarding attempt, so a create after a release does not replay the released account.
- [ ] You handle `WITHDRAWALS_HELD` and its `Retry-After`, and the 1 USDC minimum to Arc.
- [ ] You use Solana destinations until ZeroID tells you withdrawals to Arc are open in production. Your first production withdrawal to Arc is then a small amount, and you confirm it arrived before opening Arc withdrawals to users.

## Operations

- [ ] Every write sends an `Idempotency-Key`, and retries reuse it.
- [ ] You back off on `429` and `503` using `Retry-After`, and stay inside 60 requests a minute per account.
- [ ] You branch on error `code`, never on `detail`, and log `requestId`.
- [ ] You know how to suspend one account in one call, and who on your team does it.
- [ ] You know how to rotate a key (ask ZeroID for a new one, deploy it, then ask ZeroID to revoke the old one) and a webhook secret (the rotate route) without downtime.
- [ ] Your support team can help users with funding and everything else on your side, and knows to contact ZeroID support with an `accountId`, `withdrawalId` or `requestId` for anything on Kash's side.
