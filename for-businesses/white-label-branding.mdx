Your organization's markets can carry your identity instead of Kash's. Brand assets are managed in the Partner Portal under **Settings → Account**.

## What you can customise

| Element | Details |
|---|---|
| Logo | Avatar shown with your organization and markets (PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG) |
| Wordmark | Horizontal logo for wider layouts |
| Primary color | Your accent color |
| Brand font | The font used for your branded text |
| Display name | The public name shown on your surfaces |
| Tagline | A short line shown with your organization |

## Where your branding shows

To place embeds on your own site, see the [organization iframe guide](/developer-docs/public-embed-api/guides/organization-iframe) and the [thread iframe guide](/developer-docs/public-embed-api/guides/thread-iframe).

## Custom branding and your tier

Posting under your own name and X account is **custom branding**, unlocked at the **Business Partner** volume tier and above. Below that, your markets are announced through Kash's channels. See [Pricing and revenue share](/for-businesses/pricing-and-revenue-share#volume-tiers).

## Scope

Branding applies to your organization's surfaces on app.kash.bot, your embeds and your announcements. Custom domains and a standalone white-labeled app are not offered. If you need them, talk to [partnerships@kash.bot](mailto:partnerships@kash.bot).
