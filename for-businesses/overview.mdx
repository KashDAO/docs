Your audience already argues about outcomes: match results, product launches, elections, prices. On Kash you can turn those questions into live prediction markets, announce them to your followers and earn a share of the trading fees they generate. Everything happens in the Kash web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot): anyone can sign in, chat with the Kash assistant and pay to launch a market.

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Kash account">
    Sign in to the web app with X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord. See [Creating an Account](/getting-started/guides/creating-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organizations">
    A business, show or community can have an **organization** on Kash. If you're a member of one, you can launch markets for it from the web app, and those markets are shown as the organization's on Kash, with their own organization page and embeds. To set up an organization, contact [partnerships@kash.bot](mailto:partnerships@kash.bot).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Markets">
    Prediction markets with one winning outcome, from a simple yes/no to many options. Markets run on Solana, settle in USDC, and are traded on [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) and on X.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- **Market creation by chat.** Describe a market to the Kash assistant and it drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources. Kash reviews every market automatically before it goes live. See [Market creation](/for-businesses/market-creation).
- **Distribution.** Announce new markets from your connected X account and let followers trade by replying. Connect your own Discord and Telegram channels in **Settings → Distribution** so new markets are announced there too, and embed markets on any site.
- **Revenue share.** Creators earn a share of the trading fees on the markets they launch, wherever the trade happens. Earnings show in **Creator Studio** as projected, pending and paid.
- **Creator Studio.** Your private dashboard in the web app, with your submissions, market performance, audience and reach, and earnings.
- **An organization page.** Each organization has a public page on app.kash.bot listing its markets, and an embed that shows them on your own site.
