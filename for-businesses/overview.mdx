Your audience already argues about outcomes: match results, product launches, elections, prices. With a Kash organization you can turn those questions into live prediction markets, announce them to your followers, and earn a share of the trading fees they generate.

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Organization">
    Your account on Kash. It owns your markets, branding, team, connected channels, balance, earnings and analytics, all managed in the **Partner Portal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Team">
    Admins manage the organization; members can draft markets. You can require a second member to approve a market before it is submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Partners">
    Optional sub-brands you set up under your organization, such as a show, a league or a creator you work with. You can credit a market to a partner, and partner earnings are tracked separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Markets">
    Prediction markets with one winning outcome, from a simple yes/no to many options. Markets run on Solana, settle in USDC, and are traded on [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) and on X.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- **Market creation by chat.** Describe a market to the Kash AI assistant and it drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources. Every market is checked against its sources before it goes live. See [Market creation](/for-businesses/market-creation).
- **Distribution.** Announce new markets from your connected X account and to your Discord channels, and embed your markets on any site.
- **Revenue share.** Your organization earns a share of the trading fees on markets it creates, wherever the trade happens. See [Pricing and revenue share](/for-businesses/pricing-and-revenue-share).
- **Analytics.** Volume, traders, active markets and fees over time, plus an AI assistant you can ask about your numbers. See [Analytics and earnings](/for-businesses/analytics-and-earnings).
- **Branding.** Your logo, wordmark, colors and font on your organization's pages and embeds. See [White-label branding](/for-businesses/white-label-branding).

The Partner Portal itself is free. Revenue share and custom branding grow with your trading volume, and deeper intelligence is an optional add-on.
