The Partner Portal is where your organization lives on Kash: markets, balance, analytics, earnings, branding, team and channels. This page takes you from first sign-in to your first market.

## 1. Sign in with X

The portal signs you in with your **X (Twitter)** account. Use the account you'll run your organization from; you can add teammates later.

## 2. Create or join an organization

On first sign-in you can create an organization or join one with an invite link or code. Creating one asks for:

| Field | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Organization name | How your organization is named across Kash |
| Contact email | Where Kash sends account and billing notices |
| Organization URL | Your slug, which becomes your public handle on app.kash.bot |
| Logo | Optional now, editable later in **Settings → Account** |

Whoever creates the organization becomes its first admin.

## 3. Follow the setup checklist

Admins see a short checklist on the dashboard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your workspace">
    Done when your organization is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your channels">
    Connect your X account in **Settings → Platforms** so markets can be announced from it. Organizations that haven't unlocked custom branding post through Kash's own channels instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a market">
    Open **Markets** and describe your first market to the AI assistant. See [Market creation](/for-businesses/market-creation).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Before you submit a market, add USDC to your organization's balance on the **Fund** page. It pays the new market's starting liquidity.

## 4. Find your way around

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dashboard">
    Setup progress, headline numbers and recent activity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Markets">
    Create markets with the AI assistant, approve teammates' drafts in the **Queue**, and follow each request from draft to live.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Competitions">
    Competitions Kash runs with your organization, with their status and prize pot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Fund">
    Your organization's USDC balance and wallet address, with deposit and withdraw, and a record of the payments it has made.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Analytics">
    Volume, traders, markets and fees over time, plus an AI assistant for questions about your numbers. See [Analytics and earnings](/for-businesses/analytics-and-earnings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Earnings">
    Your revenue share: generated, projected, pending and paid, by market and by partner. Admins only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Insights">
    Deeper analysis and data export, part of the Community Intelligence add-on. Admins only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Share">
    Ready-to-post summaries of your performance and top markets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Settings">
    - **Account:** organization details and brand assets
    - **Team:** members, roles and invite links
    - **Platforms:** your connected X account, announcement style and delivery options
    - **Distribution:** Discord channels that receive your market announcements
    - **Partners:** the partners you work with
    - **Trade access:** allow or block specific traders on your markets
    - **Revenue share:** how fees on your markets are split
    - **Redemption assets:** extra payout options for winners besides USDC
    - **Market feed:** if Kash drafts market ideas for your organization, whether they go forward for review automatically
    - **Subscription:** your tier and the Community Intelligence add-on
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Admin | Manage the team, settings, balance, earnings and subscription, and submit markets directly |
| Member | View settings and draft markets for an admin to approve |

Invite teammates from **Settings → Team** with a link that sets their role, how many people can use it and when it expires.
