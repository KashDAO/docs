The Partner Portal is free. There is no subscription to start, run markets or earn revenue share. Your tier is earned from your trading volume, not bought, and the only paid item is the optional **Community Intelligence** add-on.

<Note>
For current tier thresholds, revenue-share levels and add-on pricing, see the **Pricing** page in the Partner Portal or contact [partnerships@kash.bot](mailto:partnerships@kash.bot).
</Note>

## What it costs

- **Using the portal:** free. Creating your organization, inviting your team, connecting channels and viewing analytics cost nothing.
- **Creating a market:** each new market needs starting liquidity in USDC, paid from your organization's balance. See [Funding a market](/for-businesses/market-creation#funding-a-market).
- **Community Intelligence:** an optional monthly add-on with deeper analytics, insights and full data export.

## Volume tiers

Your tier moves with your organization's trading volume over the trailing 30 days. Higher tiers carry a higher revenue share and unlock more:

| Tier | Highlights |
|---|---|
| Mr. Rando | Everything you need to launch and trade markets, posting through Kash's channels |
| Strategic KOL | A higher revenue share, still posting through Kash's channels |
| Business Partner | Custom branding: your own name, assets and connected X account |
| Enterprise Partner | Adds dedicated infrastructure and the option to bring your own API keys |

Every tier includes trading by mention and DM on X, shareable assets, basic analytics, trade access controls, partner management and revenue share.

## How revenue share is earned

Your organization earns a share of the trading fees on **every trade in markets it creates**. Attribution follows the market's owner, so it doesn't matter where the trade happens: on app.kash.bot, through an embed on another site, or from X.

The split depends on how a market came about, for example created by your organization directly or credited to one of your partners. You can see the splits for your organization in **Settings → Revenue share**. They are set by Kash; talk to your Kash contact to discuss them.

### From trade to payout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trades happen">
    People trade in markets your organization created, wherever they are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earnings are tracked">
    Fees are collected over the market's life and your share is recorded. You can follow it in the portal's [Earnings](/for-businesses/analytics-and-earnings#earnings) page as projected, pending and paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payouts">
    Earnings are paid in USDC to your organization's account under your revenue-share terms, after a review period.
  </Step>
</Steps>
