The Partner Portal measures the activity your markets generate and tracks what your organization earns from it.

## Analytics

The **Analytics** page covers the last 7, 30 or 90 days:

| Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Total volume | USDC traded across your markets |
| Distinct trader wallets | How many different traders took part |
| Active markets | Your markets open for trading |
| Recorded trading fees | Fees recorded on trades in your markets |

Below the headline numbers you'll find volume over time, daily traders, trade sizes, activity by weekday, comparisons with the previous period, volume by category and your top markets. You can export the daily figures as CSV.

### Ask the analytics assistant

Admins can open an AI assistant from the Analytics page and ask questions about the organization's numbers in plain language. It only reads your data, and each answer shows the figures it used.

## Insights

**Insights** is part of the **Community Intelligence** add-on and is available to admins. It adds a written weekly summary, markets to watch, market health, revenue attribution, cross-market correlation, revenue and performance breakdowns, per-market detail, and full data export as CSV or JSON.

## Earnings

The **Earnings** page, for admins, shows your revenue share:

- **Total revenue generated** by your markets
- **Projected:** your share of fees on markets still running
- **Pending payout:** earnings that are settled and waiting to be paid
- **Paid:** what has been paid to your organization

It also breaks earnings down by market and by partner, and lists recent revenue activity. See [Pricing and revenue share](/for-businesses/pricing-and-revenue-share) for how earnings are attributed and paid.
