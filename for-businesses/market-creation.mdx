Every market on Kash is created in the web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) by chatting with the Kash assistant. There is no form to fill in. If you're a member of an organization, you launch its markets from the same chat, and they go live under the organization's name.

## The lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the market">
    Tap **Launch** in the web app and describe your idea in plain language, for example "Will our team win the cup final on Saturday?". You need to be signed in with your wallet set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The assistant drafts it">
    The assistant researches the topic on the web, reads the sources it finds and checks for similar existing markets. It then drafts the question, the outcomes, the trading and resolution times, the resolution rules and the sources that will decide the result. A live preview shows the market as it takes shape. If something important is missing, the assistant asks you, and it tells you plainly what still blocks the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine by chatting">
    Ask for changes in the chat until the draft says what you mean.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the organization">
    If you belong to more than one organization, pick the one the market is for before you submit. If you belong to just one, it is chosen for you. If your organization works with partners, you can credit the market to one of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to announce it">
    Optionally announce the market on X when it goes live, either as a new post or anchored to an existing post you own, so people can trade by replying. You can also have it announced to the Discord and Telegram channels you connected in **Settings → Distribution**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund and submit">
    The launch panel shows what the launch costs before you submit. The opening USDC becomes the market's starting liquidity; it is not a fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organization approval">
    Your organization may check a market before it is sent on. While it does, the launch progress shows **Waiting for the organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kash reviews and creates the market">
    Kash reviews every market automatically before it goes live: the question must be clear, the rules sound and the result verifiable from the stated sources. If changes are needed you'll see what to change, and you can fix it in the same chat and resubmit. Once approved, Kash creates the market on Solana and announces it the way you chose. You can follow every step in the chat, and you'll be notified when it's live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits to know

- **Outcomes:** exactly one outcome wins, from a yes/no question to many options.
- **Timing:** markets need a sensible trading window, and resolution can't be too far in the future. The assistant tells you if a draft's timing doesn't fit.
- **Resolution:** the published rules and sources decide the result. You provide the rules and sources when you create the market, not the winner.

## How markets resolve

At resolution time, Kash resolves the market automatically from the sources and rules it names. A proposed result can be disputed for a short period before the market settles, and an open dispute holds settlement until it is decided. Winners then claim their payout in USDC. See [Market mechanics](/getting-started/features/market-mechanics).

## After launch

- **Creator Studio** in the web app shows your submissions, how your markets perform, their audience and reach on X, and your earnings as projected, pending and paid.
- **Your organization page** on app.kash.bot lists the organization's markets, and the [organization embed](/developer-docs/public-embed-api/guides/organization-iframe) shows them on your own site.
- **Revenue share:** creators earn a share of the trading fees on the markets they launch, wherever the trade happens. Earnings are not guaranteed.
