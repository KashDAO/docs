Organizations create markets in the Partner Portal under **Markets**. You describe the market to the Kash AI assistant, it drafts the details, and the market is checked against its sources before it goes live under your organization's name.

## The lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the market">
    Open **Markets** and start a new market. The **AI assistant** opens by default: describe your idea in plain language, for example "Will our team win the cup final on Saturday?".
  </Step>
  <Step title="The assistant drafts it">
    The assistant researches the topic on the web, reads the sources it finds and checks for similar existing markets. It then drafts the question, the outcomes, the trading and resolution times, the resolution rules and the sources that will decide the result. A live preview shows the market as it takes shape. If something important is missing, the assistant asks you, and it tells you plainly what still blocks the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine by chatting">
    Ask for changes in the chat. A draft the assistant wrote is edited in the chat; the **Form** view shows the same draft field by field. To copy an existing market and adjust it, use **Duplicate**, which opens the copy in the Form view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to announce it">
    Optionally announce the market from your organization's connected X account when it goes live, either as a new post or anchored to an existing post of yours, so people can trade by replying. You can also schedule when the market starts and when the announcement goes out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve inside your organization">
    An admin's market is submitted straight away. A member's market waits in the **Queue** tab until an admin approves it. You can also require a second member to approve, so nobody submits their own market alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kash checks the market">
    Every market is checked before it goes live: the question must be clear, the rules sound and the result verifiable from the stated sources. If changes are needed you'll see the reasons, and you can fix them in the same chat and resubmit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Market goes live on Solana">
    Once approved, the market is created on Solana, trades in USDC on [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), and is announced the way you chose.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Request statuses

Each request on the **Markets** page shows where it is:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | Still being written |
| Submitted | Being checked; nothing is needed from you yet |
| Changes requested | Edit it and resubmit |
| Approved | Queued to be created |
| Creating | Being created on-chain |
| Live | Open for trading |
| Rejected | Not accepted |
| Withdrawn | You withdrew it before creation started |
| Failed | Creation didn't complete; Kash is notified |

## Funding a market

Each new market needs starting liquidity in USDC, paid from your organization's own balance. Add funds on the **Fund** page before you submit; the portal checks your balance and shows the amount needed before you can submit. Your partners and team members never pay out of their own pockets.

## Limits to know

- **Outcomes:** exactly one outcome wins, from a yes/no question to many options.
- **Timing:** markets need a sensible trading window, and resolution can be at most a year away.
- **Resolution:** the published rules and sources decide the result. You provide the rules and sources when you create the market, not the winner.

## How markets resolve

At resolution time, Kash's resolution engine reads the sources named in the market and proposes the result. Unclear cases go to a person at Kash. After a result is proposed there is a contest window during which it can be disputed, and disputes are re-checked before the market settles. Winners then claim their payout in USDC. You can offer winners other payout options in **Settings → Redemption assets**; USDC is always available.

## Why create markets as an organization

- **Your brand** on your markets, organization page and embeds. See [White-label branding](/for-businesses/white-label-branding).
- **Your revenue:** a share of the trading fees on every market your organization creates. See [Pricing and revenue share](/for-businesses/pricing-and-revenue-share).
- **Your data:** every market feeds your organization's analytics. See [Analytics and earnings](/for-businesses/analytics-and-earnings).
