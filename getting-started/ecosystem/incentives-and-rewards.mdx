Everything below lives in the web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot). Most of it is under **Portfolio → Rewards**, which has three tabs: **XP**, **Referrals** and **Ambassadors**.

# XP, levels and achievements

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Earning XP">
    XP is awarded after qualifying predictions are held until settlement. As your XP grows, you level up. The XP tab shows your total XP, your level, your achievement count and the achievements you're closest to unlocking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Achievements">
    Achievements mark milestones across trading, accuracy, streaks, market creation, social activity and sharing. They appear on your public profile, and you can share any of them as a card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Titles">
    Some achievements and referral milestones unlock titles. Choose the one shown on your profile in **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Leaderboard

The **Community** page ranks traders by P&L, volume and win rate, and a **Top XP** view ranks them by XP. Tap any trader to open their profile, and share your own rank from the page.

# Referrals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How referrals work">
    1. Open **Portfolio → Rewards → Referrals**
    2. Copy your personal referral link
    3. Share it. A friend counts as a **qualifying referral** once they've traded a set amount, shown on the page
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Milestones">
    Referral milestones unlock as your qualifying referrals grow, from **First Referral** up through **Connector**, **Recruiter**, **Talent Scout** and **Network Builder**. Milestones can award XP, titles and badges. The page shows your progress and what each milestone gives.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Affiliate">
    Reaching the top referral milestones unlocks the **affiliate** tier: a referral commission from the protocol fees on your referrals' trades, plus a vanity link (`app.kash.bot/r/yourname`). Any commission depends on your referrals' trading and is not guaranteed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Market ambassadors

Market ambassadors are part of Kash's affiliate and referral program. Choose a market to share and you get a referral link for it. When people trade that market through your link, you receive an affiliate commission on the activity you referred, as described in the app. Any program participation requirement is shown in the app before you join. Any commission depends on the trading you refer and is not guaranteed. Manage your ambassador markets in **Portfolio → Rewards → Ambassadors**.

# Creator fees

When you [create a market](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities), you receive a creator fee: a share of the protocol fee on trades in that market. The amount depends on trading activity and is not guaranteed. **Creator Studio → Earnings** shows it as projected, pending and paid.

<Note>
Kash has **not launched a token**, and there is no token airdrop or points-for-tokens programme. Rewards on Kash are the XP, titles and badges described on this page, alongside the fees and commissions it describes.
</Note>
