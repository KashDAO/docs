Everything below lives in the web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot). Most of it is under **Portfolio → Rewards**, which has three tabs: **XP**, **Referrals** and **Ambassadors**.

# XP, levels and achievements

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Earning XP">
    XP is awarded after qualifying predictions are held until settlement. As your XP grows, you level up. The XP tab shows your total XP, your level, your achievement count and the achievements you're closest to unlocking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Achievements">
    Achievements mark milestones across trading, accuracy, streaks, market creation, social activity and sharing. They appear on your public profile, and you can share any of them as a card.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Titles">
    Some achievements and referral milestones unlock titles. Choose the one shown on your profile in **Settings**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Leaderboard

The **Community** page ranks players by P&L, volume and win rate, and a **Top XP** view ranks them by XP. Tap any player to open their profile, and share your own rank from the page.

# Referrals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How referrals work">
    1. Open **Portfolio → Rewards → Referrals**
    2. Copy your personal referral link
    3. Share it. A friend counts as a **qualifying referral** once they've traded a set amount, shown on the page
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Milestones">
    Referral milestones unlock as your qualifying referrals grow, from **First Referral** up through **Connector**, **Recruiter**, **Talent Scout** and **Network Builder**. Milestones can award XP, titles and badges, and some may include a bonus. The page shows your progress and what each milestone gives.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Affiliate">
    Reaching the top referral milestones unlocks the **affiliate** tier: a continuing share of the protocol fees from your referrals' trades, plus a vanity link (`kash.bot/r/yourname`).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Market ambassadors

Pick a market you believe in and become its ambassador. You pay a small one-time fee on that market, shown before you join, and get an ambassador link. When people trade through your link, you earn a share of the market's owner-side fee revenue from those trades. Manage your ambassador markets in **Portfolio → Rewards → Ambassadors**.

# Creator earnings

When you [create a market](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities), you earn a share of the trading fees on it. **Creator Studio → Earnings** shows your earnings as projected, pending and paid. Earnings are not guaranteed.

<Note>
Kash has **not launched a token**, and there is no token airdrop or points-for-tokens programme. Rewards on Kash are the XP, titles, badges and USDC earnings described on this page.
</Note>
