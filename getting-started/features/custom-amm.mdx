# What the AMM does

Every market has its own on-chain USDC pool that backs all of its outcomes. Prices are set by the protocol's automated market maker, a bonding-curve style pricing function: buying an outcome raises its price, and selling lowers it. There's no order book and no counterparty to match with.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Implied chance">
    The percentage shown for each outcome is its **implied chance**, and the percentages always sum to 100%. It is not a fixed share price. When a market resolves, the winning outcome's positions can be redeemed, and trade previews show an **estimated payout if this outcome wins**. See [Redeeming positions](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Price impact and slippage protection">
    Larger trades move the price more, so a big order gets a worse average rate than a small one. Every trade preview shows what you'll receive before you confirm, and if the market moves against you before execution, the trade fails instead of filling at a worse price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Fees

A protocol fee applies to buys and sells, and a small sell fee stays in the market's pool. The app shows the exact fees before you confirm.
