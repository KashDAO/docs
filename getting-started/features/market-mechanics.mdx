# Market structure

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multi-outcome markets">
    A market lists two or more outcomes, and exactly one of them wins. "Will X happen?" is a two-outcome market; "Who wins the tournament?" can list every contender.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Implied chance">
    Each outcome shows an implied chance, set by the [AMM](/getting-started/features/custom-amm) from how the market's capital is spread across outcomes. Implied chances always sum to 100%.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rules and sources">
    Every market publishes its resolution rules and a primary source (plus a fallback where needed). These decide the result, so they're shown on the market page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Market lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Creation">
    A user, an organization or Kash describes the market to Kash AI, which drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources. Kash reviews every market automatically before launch. See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live">
    The market launches on Solana with its starting liquidity in place. Anyone can buy or sell any outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Frozen">
    Trading closes a set time before the resolution time. The lead depends on the market's length: shorter for short markets, longer for long ones. The market page shows when trading closes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Waiting for resolution">
    After the resolution time, the outcome is proposed from the market's sources and rules, and a dispute window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolved or cancelled">
    With no open dispute when the window closes, the market settles to its winning outcome. A market can instead be cancelled, and then every holder is refunded at the snapshot price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Winners claim their payout, and holders of a cancelled market claim their refund, from Portfolio. See [Winning](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Step>
</Steps>

# Resolution

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How outcomes are proposed">
    Once a market reaches its resolution time, Kash resolves it automatically: the outcome is proposed from the market's published sources under its resolution rules. If the evidence isn't available yet, Kash waits and checks again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Disputes">
    A proposed outcome opens a short **dispute window**, shown on the market page. Any signed-in user can tap **Dispute outcome** and give a written reason, once per proposal. An open dispute stops settlement until the dispute has been resolved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cancellation and the refund safety net">
    A market can be cancelled when its rules require it, for example when the event doesn't take place. And if a market stays unresolved for too long after its resolution time, **anyone** can cancel it. Either way, every holder is refunded at the price snapshotted at cancellation, so live odds no longer matter once a market is cancelled.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Payouts">
    Winning shares redeem for a pro-rata portion of the market's pool. Losing shares are worth nothing. Refunds pay each holder at the cancellation snapshot price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Fees

| Action | Fee | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Buy | A protocol fee | Shared between the market's creator side and Kash |
| Sell | A protocol fee plus an AMM sell fee | The AMM sell fee stays in the market's pool |
| Trade from X | No transaction fee | |
| Trade in the web app | A small network fee in USDC may be added | Covers the Solana network fee Kash pays |
| Claim | No protocol fee | The claim's network fee is deducted from the payout |
| Launch a market | Shown in the launch panel | The creator funds the market's starting liquidity |

The app always shows the exact fees before you confirm.

# Infrastructure

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Solana and USDC">
    Markets run as programs on Solana, and every balance, trade and payout is in USDC. Your funds stay in your own Solana wallet until you trade. Kash pays the SOL network fees, so you never need to hold SOL.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slippage protection">
    Every trade is quoted before you confirm and carries an on-chain minimum-output guard. If the market moves against you before execution, the trade fails instead of filling at a worse price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
