# Trading in the web app

Open any market at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), pick an outcome, enter an amount and confirm.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Buying">
    Tap an outcome and enter your USDC amount. Before you confirm, the ticket shows:

    - **The fees**, including the protocol fee and any network fee
    - **The shares** you'll receive
    - **Estimated payout if this outcome wins**, based on the market after your trade

    Larger trades move the price more, and every trade has on-chain slippage protection.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Selling">
    You can exit any position before trading closes. The sell quote shows the sell fee (which stays in the market's pool), the protocol fee and the **net USDC you receive**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Network fee on web trades">
    Kash pays the Solana network fee for every trade. On web trades, a small network fee in USDC may be added to cover it, and the ticket shows it before you confirm. Trades placed on X skip it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Trading on X

Reply to a market's post on X, or quote it, and mention **@kash_bot_trade** with your amount and outcome, such as `buy $25 of Yes`. The trade executes from your own wallet and the bot replies with a confirmation. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).

# Copy trading

Mirror another trader automatically. From a trader's profile, start copying and set:

- **Size per trade:** how much to spend each time they buy
- **Total budget:** the most you want to spend in total

When they sell, you sell the same share of your copied position. Every copied or skipped trade is recorded with its reason, and you can pause, reset or stop copying from **Settings → Copy trading**. Your copies show in the Portfolio **Copying** tab.

# Trading through the API

Bots and agents can trade programmatically with an API key from **Settings → API keys**. Each key has scoped permissions and its own spending controls. See the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).

# Portfolio management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Positions and P&L">
    **Portfolio** shows your balance, every open position with its current value and P&L, your bookmarks, the traders you copy, and your full transaction and withdrawal history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Claiming">
    When a market resolves, winning positions appear under **Claimable**. Claim one at a time or all at once. Refunds from cancelled markets are claimed the same way. See [Redeeming positions](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sharing">
    Turn a position into a P&L card and post it to X, download it, or copy a link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
