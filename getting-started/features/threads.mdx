A thread groups related markets on one page: every question about a match, an election, a launch or a season. Instead of trading one question, you can trade your whole view of the event.

# What's in a thread

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Markets">
    Each market in the thread shows its current implied chance, predictions and volume, and the thread summary rolls up total volume, the number of markets and the time left. You can search within a thread to find a specific market.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Live chat">
    Each thread has its own chat, so the event-wide conversation happens next to the markets it's about.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Save and share">
    **Save** a thread to find it again quickly, or share it with a link. Threads show as upcoming, active or completed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Threads for partners

Threads are often created for an organization or partner, and the thread page shows who created it and who it's for. Partners can also embed a thread on their own site. See [For Businesses](/for-businesses/overview).
