# Create markets by chatting

Market creation on Kash is a conversation with **Kash AI**, not a form. You describe your prediction in plain language, for example *"Will BTC close above $100k on Friday?"*, and the assistant drafts a complete market.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your prediction">
    Connect your X account, open **Launch** in the web app and type your idea, or use voice input. Not sure what to create? The Launch page also suggests trending market ideas.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kash AI researches and drafts">
    The assistant searches the web, reads sources, checks the deadline and looks for similar existing markets. It then drafts the question, outcomes, resolution and trading close times, resolution rules, primary and fallback sources, category and a cover image.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Refine by chatting">
    Ask for any change: *"Change the resolution time to Friday 6pm UTC"*, *"Add an outcome for a draw"*, *"Use the league's official site as the source"*. Tap a fact in the draft to pre-fill an edit, and undo the last change if you don't like it. **Ready checks** flag anything that would block the launch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit">
    When the draft is ready, submit it. You pay to create the market from your own funds, at least $20, and the market is yours. Every market is reviewed automatically before it goes live, and you can follow each step (reviewing, collecting the launch amount, publishing, confirming it's live) in the same chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities) for costs, limits and what happens after launch.

<Note>
Kash AI creates markets in the web app only. It drafts the market but doesn't determine the outcome. Outcomes are determined by Kash's automated resolution system from the market's published sources and rules.
</Note>

# Talk to Kash on X

Kash also understands plain language on X:

- **Trade by replying.** Reply to a market's post, or quote it, and mention **@kash_bot_trade** with something like `buy $20 of Yes`. Kash works out the outcome and amount, submits the trade from your own wallet and replies with a confirmation. You can also ask for the current prices, the time left or your P&L on that market.
- **Ask in a DM.** Message the bot to check your balance, portfolio, P&L, trade history or pending trades, ask for prices, or discover markets. You trade by replying to market posts, not in DMs.

Replies come back in your language. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
