Kash markets are posted on X, and you can trade them without leaving X. Reply to a market's post and mention the trading account, and the trade is submitted from your own wallet.

## Before your first trade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Kash account">
    Sign up at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot). See [Creating an Account](/getting-started/guides/creating-an-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your X account">
    Sign in with X, or connect X later from **Settings → Account**. The bot recognises you by your connected X account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your wallet trading permission">
    You give this once, during onboarding. If you skipped it, the bot will tell you to finish it in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add USDC">
    Trades are paid from the USDC in your own wallet. See [Depositing](/getting-started/guides/depositing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Placing a trade

Find the market's post on X, then either **reply** to it (anywhere in its thread) or **quote** it, and mention **@kash_bot_trade** with your amount and outcome:

```
@kash_bot_trade buy $50 of Yes
```

```
@kash_bot_trade buy $10 of Argentina
```

Kash reads the amount and matches the outcome by its label, in several languages. When the trade goes through, the bot replies with a confirmation such as **"✅ Yes for $50"** and a link to the transaction.

You can also close a position the same way, or ask the bot for the current prices, the time left or your P&L on that market.

<Warning>
**Mention the account that posted the market.** Most markets are posted by [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade), but creators can post markets from their own accounts. If you mention the wrong account, the bot will tell you which one to use.
</Warning>

## Rules and limits

- **Reply to or quote the market post.** A mention on its own, a pasted market link, or a quote of a post that isn't a market won't be traded.
- **Trade size:** the bot tells you if your amount is too small to trade.
- **Act promptly:** a mention that's too old when it's processed is refused rather than filled at a stale price.
- **Open markets only:** markets that have frozen, resolved or been cancelled can't be traded.
- **No transaction fee:** trades placed on X don't carry the network fee that web trades may include. The usual protocol fee applies.

## Direct messages

You can DM the bot to:
- check your **balance**, **portfolio**, **P&L**, **trade history** and **pending trades**
- get the **prices** on a market or **discover** markets
- request an **export** of your data

Trading happens by replying to market posts, not in DMs, and markets are created in the web app.

## Your own X bot

Creators can connect their own X account in **Settings → X bot**. Kash then posts your markets from your account, and your followers can trade them by replying to your posts. Disconnecting revokes Kash's access to your X account and stops the posts.

<Note>
Kash will never DM you asking for your private key, a login code or a "verification" deposit. Deposits, withdrawals and claims happen in the web app.
</Note>
