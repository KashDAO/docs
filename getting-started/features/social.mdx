# Talk about it

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Live market chat">
    Markets and [threads](/getting-started/features/threads) have a live chat next to the prices, where you can see who's online, reply and react.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Messages">
    **Chat** is your inbox for private conversations with creators, traders and friends. You can share markets into a conversation and send USDC to the person you're chatting with. Choose who can message you (everyone, or mutual followers only) in Settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Follow and discover

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Following">
    Follow traders and creators. The **Following** tab on the home page shows their activity: markets they create, predictions they make and their results.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Profiles">
    Every user has a public profile with their prediction stats, P&L and achievements. Your track record is your reputation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Copy trading">
    Like someone's calls? Copy them. You choose the size per trade and a total budget, and their buys and sells are mirrored from your own wallet. See [Trading Options](/getting-started/features/trading-options).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Leaderboards and XP

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Leaderboard">
    The **Community** page ranks traders by P&L, volume and win rate, with a separate **Top XP** view. Share your rank straight from the page.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="XP and achievements">
    Earn XP and level up by holding qualifying predictions to settlement, and unlock achievements across trading, accuracy, streaks, market creation and sharing. See [Rewards](/getting-started/ecosystem/incentives-and-rewards).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

# Share it

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Share markets and results">
    Every market has share links for X, Telegram and Discord. Turn a position into a P&L card ("called it on Kash") and post it to X, download it or copy a link. Profiles, leaderboard ranks and achievements have shareable cards too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trade where you talk">
    Trade by replying to market posts on X, and announce the markets you create from your own X account so your followers can trade them. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Embed markets anywhere">
    Markets can be embedded on any website as a live card, so communities can bring prediction markets to their own audience. See the [Public Embed API](/developer-docs/public-embed-api/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
