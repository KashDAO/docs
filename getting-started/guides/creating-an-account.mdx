## Sign-in options

Go to the Kash web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), click **Sign in**, and choose:

| Method | How it works |
|---|---|
| **X (Twitter)** | One-click sign-in. Connecting X is also what lets you [trade from X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x) and [create markets](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities) |
| **Google** | One-click sign-in with your Google account |
| **Email** | Enter your email and confirm a one-time code |
| **Telegram** | Sign in with Telegram, including when you open the Kash web app inside Telegram |
| **Discord** | Sign in with Discord, including when you open the Kash web app inside Discord |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Any option works. You can link the others later from **Settings → Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your self-custodial wallet is created">
    You get your own **self-custodial embedded Solana wallet** automatically. It belongs to you, and it is where your USDC and positions live. There's no seed phrase to write down.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your self-custodial wallet trading permission">
    You'll be asked to **Approve Kash**, which allows Kash's app to establish a communication between your self-custodial wallet and the Kash on-chain protocol. This lets the trades you request, in the web app or on X, go through without a signing prompt each time. If you choose **Not now**, you can keep browsing and you'll be asked again when you trade. Your funds stay in your self-custodial wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a recovery method">
    Add a **recovery email** or a **passkey**. If you ever lose access to the account you signed in with, this is how you get back in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    You're ready to browse and trade. [Add funds](/getting-started/guides/depositing) now, or explore first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your self-custodial wallet works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your funds stay in your self-custodial wallet">
    - Your USDC sits in **your own self-custodial Solana wallet**. ZeroID never holds custody of your funds
    - Trades and redemptions move funds directly between your self-custodial wallet and the on-chain market
    - You can **export your private key** from **Settings → Account → Wallet backup** and import it into a compatible Solana wallet. ZeroID never sees your private key
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No seed phrase, real recovery">
    - Access is tied to your sign-in methods
    - Add a recovery email or passkey, and link more than one sign-in method, so losing one doesn't lock you out
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No SOL needed">
    - ZeroID pays the Solana network fees for you
    - On some actions a small network fee is charged back in USDC, and the web app shows it before you confirm
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Keep your account safe

- **Turn on two-factor authentication** with your sign-in provider (X, Google, Telegram, Discord or your email account)
- **Add a recovery method** during onboarding, and don't skip it
- **Never share** login codes or your exported private key with anyone. ZeroID will never ask for them
