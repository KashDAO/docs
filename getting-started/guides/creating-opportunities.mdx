Anyone with a Kash account can launch a market. There's no form to fill in: you describe the market to **Kash AI**, the assistant drafts it, and you refine it by chatting.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Launch">
    Tap **Launch** at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) and describe your idea in plain language, for example "Will ETH close above $5,000 on December 31?". You need to be signed in with your wallet set up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Kash AI drafts the market">
    The assistant researches the topic and drafts:
    - **Question:** the title the market answers
    - **Outcomes:** the possible answers
    - **Resolution time** and **trading close time**
    - **Resolution rules:** what counts as each outcome
    - **Sources:** a primary source, plus a fallback if needed
    - **Category** and a **cover image**

    It also checks for similar existing markets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine by chatting">
    Ask for changes in the chat: "change the resolution time to Friday 6pm UTC", "add an outcome", "use a different source". Tapping any fact in the draft pre-fills an edit for you. The draft shows **Blockers** to fix and **Warnings** worth a look before you submit. You can also upload your own cover image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to launch">
    Launch on Kash only, or on Kash and X. With X, the market is announced from **your own connected X account** when it goes live, either as a new post or by anchoring it to an existing post of yours. You can schedule the X post for later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund and submit">
    The launch panel shows the exact amount before you submit. This USDC is the market's **starting liquidity**, not a fee, and it is not returned to you. When available, Kash may cover some or all of it as a free launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Kash reviews every market automatically before it goes live, checking that the question is clear, the rules are sound and the result can be verified from the stated sources. If a market is sent back, you'll see why and what to change, and you can revise it in the same chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Live">
    Once approved, the market launches on Solana and appears on Kash for everyone to trade. You can follow progress in the chat and in **Creator Studio → Submissions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Market creation happens in the web app only. Messaging the bot on X can't create a market.
</Note>

## Limits to know

- **Outcomes:** each market picks exactly one winning outcome, from a yes/no question to many options.
- **Timing:** the resolution time can't be too soon or too far in the future; the assistant tells you if a draft's timing doesn't fit. Trading closes a set time before resolution, depending on the market's length.
- **Resolution:** the published sources and rules decide the result. Creating a market doesn't let you choose the winner.

## Creator Studio and earnings

**Creator Studio** is your private dashboard, with your submissions, audience and reach, and earnings. Creators earn a share of the trading fees on the markets they launch. Referral or ambassador shares come out of the creator's side. Earnings appear in Creator Studio as projected, pending and paid, and they are not guaranteed.

## Share it

After launch you get share links for X, Telegram and Discord. You can also:
- **Announce later** on X if you launched quietly
- Connect your own **Discord** and **Telegram** channels in **Settings → Distribution** so new markets are announced there automatically
- Set up your **X bot** in **Settings → X bot** so followers can trade your markets by replying to your posts

## What makes a good market

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Essentials">
    - **A precise question:** "Will BTC close above $150,000 on March 1?" beats "Will Bitcoin moon?"
    - **Mutually exclusive outcomes:** exactly one should be able to win
    - **A verifiable result:** resolvable from a public, reliable source
    - **A sensible resolution time:** soon enough to stay interesting, late enough that the answer is knowable
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What traders like">
    Timely questions on public events with objective outcomes (sports results, elections, economic data, product launches, crypto milestones) attract the most volume.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Creating markets for an organization

If you're a member of an organization on Kash, you can launch a market under its name. The organization may check the market before it is sent on. See [For Businesses](/for-businesses/market-creation).

<Tip>
The best markets ask questions people already argue about, with resolution rules nobody can argue about.
</Tip>
