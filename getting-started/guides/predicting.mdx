# Understanding market odds

## How prediction markets work

Every market shows an **implied chance** for each outcome: the market's current estimate of how likely it is, based on real money traded.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Implied chance">
    - **Yes at 72%** means the market currently implies a 72% chance the event happens
    - The implied chance moves as people trade and new information emerges
    - Buying an outcome pushes its price up; selling pushes it down
    - Markets can have more than two outcomes, and the same logic applies to each one
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your potential returns">
    - The trade ticket shows an **estimated payout if you win** before you confirm
    - The lower the price when you buy, the higher your estimated payout if you're right
    - The estimate can shift as others trade. Your final payout is your share of the market's pool at resolution
    - If your outcome loses, the position is worth nothing at resolution
    - You can sell at the current price any time before trading closes
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Market dynamics">
    - **Early predictions** often get better prices before consensus forms
    - **Breaking news** can shift prices quickly
    - **Large trades** move prices more than small ones
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# How to make predictions

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In the web app">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find a market">
        Browse the home feed, categories or search on [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick an outcome">
        Tap the outcome you believe in. The trade ticket opens.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set your amount">
        Enter how much USDC to spend. The ticket shows the fees and your **estimated payout if you win**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Confirm the trade. It executes from your Kash wallet, and the position appears in your Portfolio.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="On X">
    Reply to a market's post on X, or quote it, and mention **@kash_bot_trade** with your amount and outcome:

    ```
    @kash_bot_trade $50 on Yes
    ```

    The bot places the trade from your Kash wallet and replies with a confirmation and a transaction link. Your Kash account must be linked to X first. Trades on X carry no transaction fee. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x) for the full rules.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Resolution">
    - Trading closes shortly before the market's resolution time, and positions are locked in
    - After the resolution time, the outcome is proposed from the market's sources and rules, followed by a short dispute window
    - Winning positions then become **claimable in your Portfolio**
    - If a market is cancelled, positions are refunded at the snapshot price

    See [Winning](/getting-started/guides/winning) for how payouts, claims and refunds work.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

# Prediction strategies

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Information edge">
    - **Read the rules and sources** on the market page: they decide the result
    - **Research before predicting**: news, trends, context
    - **Spot mispricing**: implied chances that don't match reality
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Portfolio approach">
    - **Diversify** across markets
    - **Don't trade your entire balance**
    - **Size predictions** to your confidence
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Social signals">
    - **Watch strong predictors** on the leaderboard and follow them
    - **Copy a trader** you trust from their profile. See [Trading Options](/getting-started/features/trading-options)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Managing your positions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Track your positions">
    - **Portfolio** shows every open position, its current value and live P&L
    - Plan your exit: sell early or hold to resolution
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sell before trading closes">
    - Lock in profits or cut losses at the current price
    - Sell from the market page or your Portfolio
    - Sells pay a protocol fee plus an AMM sell fee that stays in the market
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add to a position">
    - Buy the same outcome again to increase your stake
    - Adding at a different price averages your entry
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Start small while you learn how prices move. The ticket shows every fee before you confirm.
</Tip>
