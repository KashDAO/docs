# How deposits work

Everything on Kash runs on **USDC** (a US dollar stablecoin) on **Solana**. Deposits land directly in your own wallet.

- **USDC on Solana only.** Your balance is held in USDC on Solana.
- **No deposit fee from Kash.** Payment providers may charge their own fees, and the checkout shows them before you pay.
- **No SOL needed.** You don't need to hold SOL to receive, trade or withdraw.

# Deposit methods

Open **Add funds** from your wallet or Portfolio on [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot).

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Checkout">
    **Buy or transfer USDC through the checkout**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Add funds">
        The checkout opens by default when you tap **Add funds**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose an amount and payment method">
        Pick an amount and one of the payment methods offered. The available methods depend on your region and are shown in the checkout.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Fees and the amount you'll receive are shown before you confirm. The USDC is delivered to your wallet on Solana.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    The checkout is run by a third-party payment provider, and Kash never sees or stores your card or bank details. The provider may ask you to verify your identity.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Send USDC on Solana">
    **Send USDC from an exchange or an external Solana wallet**

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Get your address">
        In **Add funds**, choose **USDC**. Kash shows your wallet address and a QR code.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send USDC on Solana">
        From your exchange or wallet, withdraw **USDC** on the **Solana** network to that address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
        Your balance updates once the transfer confirms on Solana.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
    Send **only USDC on Solana** to this address. Other tokens, or USDC sent on a different network, will not be credited and may be unrecoverable.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

# After your deposit

- **Your balance updates automatically** once the deposit confirms
- **You can trade immediately**, with no waiting period
- **Your history** is in **Portfolio → Transactions**
- **Withdraw any time.** There are no lock-ups. See [Withdrawing](/getting-started/guides/withdrawing)

<Tip>
Start with a small first deposit to get comfortable with the flow.
</Tip>
