## The home feed

The home page at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) has two tabs:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For you">
    A feed of markets picked for you, with rails such as **Trending markets**, **Markets coming soon** and **Featured creators**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Following">
    Markets and activity from the creators and traders you follow: new markets, results and predictions.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Narrow it down

- **Explore:** browse live markets by category, creator, activity and momentum
- **Categories:** each category has its own page of markets
- **Search:** find markets, creators and traders by keyword, or check what's trending and ending soon
- **Bookmarks:** save a market and find it later in your Portfolio

## Creators and threads

- **Creator profiles** collect the markets a creator has made. Follow a creator to see their new markets in your Following feed.
- **[Threads](/getting-started/features/threads)** group related markets on one page, so you can trade your whole view of an event.

## Can't find the market you want?

Create it. Connect your X account and describe it to Kash AI on the **Launch** page. See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities).
