## The home feed

The home page at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) has two tabs:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For you">
    A feed of markets picked for you, with rails such as **Trending markets**, **Markets coming soon**, featured creators and top earners.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Following">
    Markets and activity from the creators, traders and organizations you follow: new launches, wins and predictions.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Narrow it down

- **Explore:** browse live markets by category, creator, activity and momentum
- **Categories:** each category has its own page of markets
- **Search:** find markets, players and organizations by keyword, or check what's trending and ending soon
- **Bookmarks:** save a market and find it later in your Portfolio

## Organizations and threads

- **Organization pages** collect every market an organization has launched, plus its traders and followers. Follow an organization to see its new markets in your Following feed.
- **[Threads](/getting-started/features/threads)** group related markets on one page, so you can trade your whole view of an event.

## Can't find the market you want?

Launch it. Describe it to Kash AI on the **Launch** page. See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities).

## Finding your edge

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Trade what you know">
    Markets where you have real knowledge (your industry, your sport, your local scene) are where you're most likely to spot mispriced odds.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Watch the timing">
    New markets and pre-event windows often have prices that haven't caught up with available information.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Read the market page">
    Every market page shows its resolution rules and sources. They decide the result, so read them before you trade.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
