# Claiming your winnings

## When a market resolves

1. **The outcome is proposed** from the market's published sources and rules.
2. **A short dispute window** follows. Any signed-in user can dispute the proposed outcome, and an open dispute holds settlement until it has been resolved.
3. **The market settles** on-chain, and winning positions appear under **Claimable** in your Portfolio.
4. **You claim.** Tap **Claim winnings** on a position, or **Claim all**, and your winning shares are redeemed for USDC in your wallet.

<Note>
A claim covers its own small network fee, which is deducted from the payout. Your first claim also covers setting up your USDC account. There is no Kash fee on claims.
</Note>

## When a market is cancelled

A market can be cancelled when its rules require it (for example, the event is called off) or when it can't be resolved. You claim the refund the same way: tap **Claim refund** in your Portfolio.

- **Refund value:** every holder is refunded at the market price **snapshotted at cancellation**, not your entry price. If you bought at 30¢ and the snapshot price was 45¢, you're refunded 45¢ per share, and vice versa.
- **Trading fees you already paid are not refunded.**

<Note>
**A refund path that doesn't depend on Kash:** if a market stays unresolved for too long after its resolution time, *anyone* can cancel it, and every holder becomes eligible for a refund at snapshot prices.
</Note>

# Understanding payouts

## How payouts are calculated

Winning shares don't pay a fixed $1. They redeem for a **share of the market's pool**, the USDC that traders paid in:

- **Your payout = your winning shares ÷ all winning shares × the market's pool**
- The trade ticket shows an **estimated payout if you win** at the moment you trade
- The estimate can shift as others trade, because the pool and share supply are only fixed at resolution

## Fees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Fee schedule">
    - **Buys:** a protocol fee
    - **Sells:** a protocol fee plus an AMM sell fee, which stays in the market's pool
    - **Trades on X:** no transaction fee
    - **Trades in the web app:** a small network fee in USDC may be added
    - **Claims:** no Kash fee; the claim's network fee is deducted from the payout
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When fees are charged">
    - Trading fees are charged **when you trade**
    - Holding a position costs nothing
    - Kash charges no deposit fee; payment providers may charge their own
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payout example

```
You spend $100 on Yes when the implied chance is 40%.
The trade ticket shows: "Estimated payout if you win: ~$240"

The market resolves Yes.
Your position appears under Claimable in Portfolio.
You claim and receive your share of the market's pool:
close to the estimate, adjusted for trading after your entry.
```

# Ways a position can end

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Market win">
    Your outcome won. Your shares redeem for your share of the pool. Claim from your Portfolio.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Early exit">
    You sold before trading closed and received USDC straight away, with no claim needed. Sells pay the protocol fee plus the AMM sell fee.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Market loss">
    Your outcome didn't win, and the shares are worth nothing at resolution. This is why position sizing matters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Market cancelled">
    Claim your refund from Portfolio at the cancellation snapshot price.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Managing positions and risk

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Position sizing">
    - Never risk more than you can afford to lose completely
    - Spread across markets instead of going all-in on one
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Exit timing">
    - You don't have to wait for resolution. Selling early locks in gains and removes resolution risk
    - Factor the sell fees into exit decisions
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
