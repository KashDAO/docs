# Withdrawal overview

Your balance is USDC on Solana in your own wallet, and you can withdraw it at any time. There are no lock-ups, and you don't need SOL to pay the network fee.

# How to withdraw

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the withdrawal flow">
    On [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), open your wallet or Portfolio and choose **Withdraw**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the amount and the destination: an external wallet or an exchange deposit address. The withdrawal checkout may also let you receive on another network. Choose carefully and match the network your destination expects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the amount, destination, network and any fees, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the status">
    You can follow the status in the app and in **Portfolio → Withdrawals**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If a withdrawal is interrupted, it clears automatically and you can try again.
</Note>

## Withdrawing to an exchange

1. Find your **USDC deposit address** on the exchange and check which network it expects.
2. Paste the address into the Kash withdrawal flow and select the **matching network**.
3. Confirm, and allow time for the exchange to credit you.

<Warning>
Sending to an address on the wrong network, or to an address that doesn't accept USDC, can result in permanent loss. When in doubt, send a small test amount first.
</Warning>

## Sending USDC to another Kash user

You can send USDC to another Kash user from a chat in **Messages**. If you can message someone, you can pay them. A small network fee may be added, and the app shows it before you confirm.

## Security best practices

- Double-check every address before confirming
- Use addresses you control, and save the ones you use often
- Kash will never ask you to send funds to "verify" your account

# Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My withdrawal is taking longer than expected">
    Check its status in **Portfolio → Withdrawals**. Exchange credits can take longer than the on-chain transfer itself. If it still looks stuck, contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) with the amount, destination and time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I sent to the wrong address">
    On-chain transfers can't be reversed. If you sent to an exchange on the wrong network, contact that exchange's support, as they can sometimes recover it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
