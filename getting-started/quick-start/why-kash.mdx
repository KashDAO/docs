Prediction markets are a great idea with a rough onboarding problem: wallets, gas tokens, seed phrases, order books. Kash removes that friction without pooling your money.

## The differentiators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="App-like experience">
    Sign in with X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord. A Solana wallet is created for you, with no seed phrase to write down and no browser extension. Kash pays the network fees, so you never need SOL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trade where the conversation is">
    Markets are posted on X, and you can trade them by replying to the post. No app switch, no link to open. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Anyone can launch a market">
    Describe a question to Kash AI and it drafts a complete market: outcomes, timing, resolution rules and sources. Every market is reviewed before it goes live, and creators earn a share of its trading fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multi-outcome markets with instant liquidity">
    A market can list many outcomes, each priced by an automated market maker funded at launch. You can enter or exit whenever trading is open. See [Custom AMM](/getting-started/features/custom-amm).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your own wallet">
    Your USDC stays in your own Solana wallet rather than a Kash pool, and you can export the private key from Settings at any time. Kash can place trades for you only after you approve it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transparent resolution and a refund path">
    Outcomes are proposed from each market's published sources and rules, and anyone can dispute a proposed result before it settles. If a market is still unresolved a day after its resolution time, anyone can cancel it and every holder is refunded.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## At a glance

| | Typical platforms | Kash |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | Wallet setup, seed phrases | Social login, wallet created for you |
| **Network fees** | Paid by you in the chain's token | Paid by Kash; you never need SOL |
| **Custody** | Platform holds funds | Your own wallet |
| **Market structure** | Often binary only | Multiple outcomes per market |
| **Where you trade** | One app | Web app and X |
| **Stuck markets** | At the platform's discretion | Anyone can trigger a refund after the grace period |
| **Fees** | Varies | 1% on buys; 1% + 0.5% on sells |

The app always shows the exact fees before you confirm.
