Prediction markets are a great idea with a rough onboarding problem: wallets, gas tokens, seed phrases, order books. Kash removes that friction, and never takes custody of your money.

## The differentiators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="App-like experience">
    Sign in with X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord. You get your own embedded Solana wallet, with no seed phrase to write down and no browser extension. Kash pays the network fees, so you never need SOL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Trade where the conversation is">
    Markets are posted on X, and you can trade them by replying to the post. No app switch, no link to open. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Anyone can create a market">
    Connect your X account and describe a question to Kash AI, and it drafts a complete market: outcomes, timing, resolution rules and sources. Every market is reviewed automatically before it goes live. The creator pays to create it from their own funds, at least $20, owns the market, and earns a share of its trading fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multi-outcome markets with instant liquidity">
    A market can list many outcomes, each priced by an on-chain automated market maker funded when the market is created. You can enter or exit whenever trading is open. See [Custom AMM](/getting-started/features/custom-amm).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Non-custodial: your own wallet">
    Markets are created and traded on an open, permissionless protocol on Solana. Kash provides the interface and operates the automated market creation and resolution system. Your USDC and positions stay in your own Solana wallet, Kash never holds custody of them, and you can export the private key from Settings at any time. To trade from the app, chat and X, you approve Kash's app to submit transactions from your wallet on your behalf.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transparent resolution and a refund path">
    Outcomes are determined by Kash's automated resolution system from each market's published sources and rules. Any signed-in user can dispute a proposed result before it settles, and the same automated system reviews the dispute. If a market can't be resolved after its resolution time, anyone can cancel it and every holder is refunded.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## At a glance

| | Typical platforms | Kash |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboarding** | Wallet setup, seed phrases | Social login, your own embedded wallet |
| **Network fees** | Paid by you in the chain's token | Paid by Kash; you never need SOL |
| **Custody** | Often held by the platform | Non-custodial: your own wallet; Kash never holds custody |
| **Market structure** | Often binary only | Multiple outcomes per market |
| **Where you trade** | One app | Web app and X |
| **Stuck markets** | At the platform's discretion | Anyone can cancel an unresolved market after its resolution time, through the protocol |
| **Fees** | Varies | A protocol fee on trades, shown before you confirm |

The app always shows the exact fees before you confirm.
