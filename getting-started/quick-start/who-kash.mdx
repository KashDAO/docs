<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Traders">
    **Anyone with an opinion about a future event**

    - Trade multi-outcome markets on sports, crypto, politics, tech and entertainment
    - Start small: no trading experience required
    - Trade in the web app or straight from X
    - Track P&L in your [Portfolio](/getting-started/guides/winning), climb the leaderboard, earn XP and achievements, and [copy other traders](/getting-started/features/trading-options)

    Sign-in takes seconds, you never need SOL for network fees, and your funds stay in your own wallet.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Market creators">
    **People who spot questions worth trading**

    - Describe a market to Kash AI and it drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources
    - Fund the market's starting liquidity, and it goes live after review
    - Announce it from your own X account and let followers trade in the replies
    - Earn a share of the trading fees your markets generate, tracked in Creator Studio

    See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Organizations & partners">
    **Communities, media and brands**

    - Launch markets under your organization's name, with your own review step
    - Get a branded organization page with your markets, traders and followers
    - Embed markets on your own site
    - Earn revenue share on trades in your markets

    Start at [For Businesses](/for-businesses/overview).
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Developers">
    **Builders integrating prediction markets**

    - Public REST API for market data, portfolios and trading, with webhooks
    - TypeScript and Python SDKs, plus a CLI
    - Public embed API for read-only market widgets

    Start at the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What makes it accessible

- **No crypto expertise needed:** sign in with X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord, and a wallet is created for you.
- **No SOL needed:** Kash pays the Solana network fees.
- **Your own wallet:** funds live in your wallet, not in a Kash pool, and you can export its key.
- **Global, 24/7:** available worldwide except in restricted regions.
