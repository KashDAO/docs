Kash is a prediction market. You buy shares in the outcome of a real-world event (a match result, a price target, an election, a product launch) and, if you're right, you claim your share of the market's pool when it resolves.

Markets run on **Solana**, and everything is priced and settled in **USDC**. You trade at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), or by replying to a market's post on X with [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade).

---

## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multi-outcome markets">
    A market isn't limited to Yes/No. "Who wins the final?" can list every plausible answer, each with its own live price. A price reflects the market's implied chance for that outcome.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your own wallet, no SOL needed">
    When you sign in, Kash creates a Solana wallet for you. Your USDC stays in that wallet, not in a Kash pool, and you can export its private key at any time. Kash pays the Solana network fees, so you never need to hold SOL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Instant liquidity">
    Every market trades against an automated market maker (AMM) that is funded when the market launches. You can buy or sell whenever trading is open. There's no order book and no waiting for a counterparty.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Markets created by chatting with Kash AI">
    Anyone with an account can launch a market by describing it to **Kash AI**. The assistant drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources, and every market is reviewed before it goes live.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transparent resolution with a dispute window">
    When a market ends, its outcome is proposed from the published sources and rules, then opens to a short dispute window before it settles. If a market can't be resolved, it is cancelled and every holder is refunded. See [Market Mechanics](/getting-started/features/market-mechanics).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fees

| Action | Fee |
|---|---|
| Buy an outcome | 1% protocol fee |
| Sell an outcome | 1% protocol fee + 0.5% AMM sell fee (kept in the market) |
| Trade from X | No transaction fee |
| Trade on the web | A small network fee in USDC may be added |
| Launch a market | No creation fee. You fund the market's starting liquidity |

The app always shows the exact fees before you confirm. See [Transactions](/getting-started/faqs/transactions) for details.

## Where you can use it

- **The web app** at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot): markets, your portfolio, Kash AI market creation, chat, leaderboards and rewards.
- **X (Twitter):** trade by replying to a market's post. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
- **Partner sites:** organizations can embed Kash markets on their own pages.
- **Developers:** a REST API, SDKs and a CLI. See the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).
