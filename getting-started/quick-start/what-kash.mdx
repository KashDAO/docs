Kash is a place to trade predictions on real-world outcomes. You buy shares in an outcome of an event (a match result, a price target, an election, a product launch) and, if that outcome happens, your shares are redeemable for USDC when the market resolves.

Everything is priced and settled in **USDC** on **Solana**. You trade at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), or by replying to a market's post on X with [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade).

## On-chain and non-custodial

- **The markets live on an open protocol.** Every market is created and traded on a decentralized, permissionless on-chain protocol on Solana that anyone can use directly.
- **Kash provides the interface and runs market creation and resolution.** Kash is an interface built on that protocol: the Kash web app and the X bot let you use it without dealing with the chain directly. Kash also operates the automated system that creates markets and determines their outcomes.
- **Kash never holds your funds.** Your USDC and your positions sit in your own wallet, and you can export its private key. So that you can trade from the app, chat and X without a signing prompt each time, you approve Kash's app to submit transactions from your wallet on your behalf. Kash never takes custody of user funds, at any point.
- **You own what you create.** Your wallet, your positions and the markets you create are yours.

## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multi-outcome markets">
    A market isn't limited to Yes/No. "Who wins the final?" can list every plausible answer, each with its own live price. A price reflects the market's implied chance for that outcome.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your own wallet, no SOL needed">
    When you sign in, you get your own embedded Solana wallet. You own it: your USDC stays in it, and you can export its private key at any time to use the wallet anywhere else. Kash pays the Solana network fees, so you never need to hold SOL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Instant liquidity">
    Every market trades against the protocol's on-chain automated market maker (AMM), which is funded when the market is created and sets prices: buying an outcome raises its price, and selling lowers it. You can buy or sell whenever trading is open. There's no order book and no waiting for a counterparty.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Markets created by chatting with Kash AI">
    Anyone can create a market: sign in at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), connect your X account and describe the market to **Kash AI**. The assistant drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources, and every market is reviewed automatically before it goes live. You pay to create it from your own funds, at least $20, and the market is yours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transparent resolution with a dispute window">
    When a market ends, Kash's automated resolution system proposes its outcome from the published sources and rules, then a short dispute window opens before it settles. Any disputes are reviewed by the same automated system. If a market can't be resolved, it is cancelled and every holder is refunded. See [Market Mechanics](/getting-started/features/market-mechanics).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fees

A protocol fee applies to buys and sells, and a small sell fee stays in the market's pool. Creating a market costs at least $20. This provides the market's initial liquidity and is not returned to the creator. The app always shows the exact fees before you confirm. See [Transactions](/getting-started/faqs/transactions) for details.

## Where you can use it

- **The web app** at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot): markets, your portfolio, Kash AI market creation, chat, leaderboards and rewards.
- **X (Twitter):** trade by replying to a market's post. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
- **Embeds:** any site can show live Kash markets.
- **Developers:** a REST API, SDKs and a CLI. See the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).
