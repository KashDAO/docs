Kash is a protocol for trading predictions on real-world outcomes. You buy shares in an outcome of an event (a match result, a price target, an election, a product launch) and, if that outcome happens, your shares are redeemable for USDC when the market resolves.

Everything is priced and settled in **USDC** on **Solana**. You trade through the Kash web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), or by replying to a market's post on X with [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade). The web app and the X bot are operated by ZeroID Labs Ltd. ("ZeroID"), the company behind Kash.

## On-chain and non-custodial

- **The markets live on an open protocol.** Every market is created and traded on the Kash protocol, a decentralized, permissionless on-chain protocol on Solana that anyone can use directly.
- **The web app is an interface to the protocol.** The Kash web app and the X bot let you use the protocol without dealing with the chain directly.
- **ZeroID never holds your funds.** Your USDC and your positions sit in your own self-custodial wallet, and you can export its private key. All transactions are submitted from your self-custodial wallet to the protocol. ZeroID never takes custody of user funds, at any point.
- **You own what you create.** Your self-custodial wallet, your positions and the markets you create are yours.

## The basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Multi-outcome markets">
    A market isn't limited to Yes/No. "Who wins the final?" can list every plausible answer, each with its own live price. A price reflects the market's implied chance for that outcome.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your own self-custodial wallet, no SOL needed">
    When you sign in, you get your own self-custodial embedded Solana wallet. You own it: your USDC stays in it, and you can export its private key at any time to use the self-custodial wallet anywhere else. ZeroID pays the Solana network fees, so you never need to hold SOL.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Instant liquidity">
    Every market trades against the protocol's on-chain automated market maker (AMM), which is funded when the market is created and sets prices: buying an outcome raises its price, and selling lowers it. You can buy or sell whenever trading is open. There's no order book and no waiting for a counterparty.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Markets created by chatting with Kash AI">
    Anyone can create a market: sign in at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), connect your X account and describe the market to **Kash AI**. The assistant drafts the question, outcomes, timing, rules and sources, and every market is reviewed automatically before it goes live. You pay to create it from your own funds, at least $20, and the market is yours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Transparent resolution with a dispute window">
    When a market ends, Kash's automated resolution system proposes its outcome from the published sources and rules, then a short dispute window opens before it settles. Any disputes are reviewed by the same automated system. If a market can't be resolved, it is cancelled and every holder is refunded. See [Market Mechanics](/getting-started/features/market-mechanics).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Fees

A protocol fee applies to buys and sells, and a small sell fee stays in the market's pool. Creating a market costs at least $20. This provides the market's initial liquidity and is not returned to the creator. The web app always shows the exact fees before you confirm. See [Transactions](/getting-started/faqs/transactions) for details.

## Where you can use it

- **The web app** at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot): markets, your portfolio, Kash AI market creation, chat, leaderboards and rewards.
- **X (Twitter):** trade by replying to a market's post. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
- **Embeds:** any site can show live markets from the Kash protocol.
- **Developers:** a REST API, SDKs and a CLI. See the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).
