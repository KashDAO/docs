Markets trade around the clock. The clock that matters is each market's own **resolution time**, shown on every market.

## The life of a market

<Steps>
  <Step title="Live: trading is open">
    From launch until shortly before its resolution time, you can buy and sell any outcome. Prices move with every trade, so the odds reflect the latest information.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Frozen: trading closes">
    Trading closes a set time before the resolution time. The lead depends on the market's length: shorter for short markets, longer for long ones. The market page shows when trading closes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Waiting for resolution">
    Once the resolution time passes, the outcome is proposed from the market's published sources and rules. A short dispute window follows, during which any signed-in user can challenge the proposed result.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Resolved: winnings become claimable">
    When the window closes without an open dispute, the market settles. Winning positions become claimable in your [Portfolio](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Step>

  <Step title="If a market can't resolve: refunds">
    A market can be cancelled when its rules require it, or if it stays unresolved for too long after its resolution time. Every holder is then refunded at the snapshot price taken at cancellation, and a **Claim refund** button appears in your Portfolio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Picking your moment

- **Early positioning:** new markets and scheduled events often offer better prices before the crowd arrives.
- **When news breaks:** prices adjust as information spreads. If you know something the market hasn't priced in yet, that's your edge.
- **Any time before the freeze:** you can enter or exit whenever trading is open. You don't have to hold until resolution.

<Tip>
Each market runs to its own resolution time. Check the countdown and the trading close time on the market page before you trade.
</Tip>
