## The core loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) and sign in with **X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord**. A Solana wallet is created for you automatically, with no seed phrase to write down. You'll also approve Kash to place trades from that wallet. See [Creating an Account](/getting-started/guides/creating-an-account).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add USDC">
    Open **Add funds** and use the checkout, or send USDC on Solana to your wallet address. See [Depositing](/getting-started/guides/depositing).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find a market">
    Browse the home feed, explore by category, or search. See [Finding Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/finding-opportunities).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy an outcome">
    On a market page, pick an outcome, enter an amount, review the quote and confirm. See [Predicting](/getting-started/guides/predicting).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track your positions">
    **Portfolio** shows your open positions with live P&L, your history and your balance. You can sell out of a position any time before trading closes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Claim when the market resolves">
    When a market resolves, winning positions show up as claimable in your Portfolio. Tap **Claim winnings**. If a market is cancelled, tap **Claim refund** instead. See [Winning](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Trade from X

Once your Kash account is linked to X, you can trade without opening the app: reply to a market's post (or quote it) and mention **@kash_bot_trade** with your amount and outcome, for example `$10 on Yes`. The bot places the trade from your Kash wallet and replies with a confirmation. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).

## Launch your own market

Open **Launch** in the app and describe the market you want to Kash AI. The assistant drafts it, you refine it by chatting, and it goes live once Kash's automatic review approves it. See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities).
