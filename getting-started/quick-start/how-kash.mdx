## The core loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot) and sign in with **X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord**. You get your own embedded Solana wallet automatically, with no seed phrase to write down. You also give the wallet permission for Kash to submit the trades you request, such as the ones you place by replying on X. See [Creating an Account](/getting-started/guides/creating-an-account).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add USDC">
    Open **Add funds** and use the checkout, or send USDC on Solana to your wallet address. See [Depositing](/getting-started/guides/depositing).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find a market">
    Browse the home feed, explore by category, or search. See [Finding Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/finding-opportunities).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy an outcome">
    On a market page, pick an outcome, enter an amount, review the quote and confirm. See [Predicting](/getting-started/guides/predicting).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track your positions">
    **Portfolio** shows your open positions with live P&L, your history and your balance. You can sell out of a position any time before trading closes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Claim when the market resolves">
    When a market resolves, winning positions show up as claimable in your Portfolio. Claim them to redeem for USDC in your wallet. If a market is cancelled, tap **Claim refund** instead. See [Redeeming positions](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Trade from X

Once your Kash account is connected to X, you can trade without opening the app: reply to a market's post (or quote it) and mention **@kash_bot_trade** with your amount and outcome, for example `buy $10 of Yes`. The trade is submitted from your own wallet, and the bot replies with a confirmation. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).

## Create your own market

Connect your X account in the app, open **Launch** and describe the market you want to Kash AI. The assistant drafts it and you refine it by chatting. You pay to create it from your own funds, at least $20, and it goes live once the automatic review approves it. The market is yours. See [Creating Opportunities](/getting-started/guides/creating-opportunities).
