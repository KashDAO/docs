## The web app: app.kash.bot

**[app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot)** is where you manage everything:

- Browse, search and trade markets
- Connect your X account and create your own markets with Kash AI
- Track positions and redeem winning ones in your [Portfolio](/getting-started/guides/winning)
- Add and withdraw USDC
- Chat with other traders, follow creators, climb the leaderboard and earn XP

It works in desktop and mobile browsers, and you can install it to your home screen as an app.

## On X (Twitter)

Kash markets are posted on X, and you can trade them right there. Reply to a market's post (or quote it) and mention **[@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade)** with your amount and outcome. You can also DM the bot to check your balance, portfolio and prices. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).

<Warning>
Kash's official accounts are [@kash_bot](https://x.com/kash_bot) for news and [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade) for trading. Creators can also post markets from their own accounts, and each market's post tells you which account to mention. Kash will never DM you asking for your private key.
</Warning>

## Inside Telegram and Discord

You can open Kash inside Telegram and Discord and sign in with that account. Creators can also connect their own Discord and Telegram channels so new markets are announced there.

## Embedded on other sites

Any site can embed live Kash markets with the no-key [Public Embed API](/developer-docs/public-embed-api/overview).

## For developers

Kash has a public REST API, SDKs and a CLI for building on the platform, from market data to programmatic trading. Start at the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).

---

## Availability

- **24/7:** markets trade around the clock.
- **Not available everywhere:** availability, eligibility and any verification requirements are set out in the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use).
- **On-chain:** markets live on an open, permissionless protocol on Solana, and trades settle there, so activity is verifiable. Kash never holds custody of your funds.
