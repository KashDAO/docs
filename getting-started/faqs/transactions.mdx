# Fees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What fees does Kash charge?">
    | Action | Fee |
    |---|---|
    | Buy | A protocol fee |
    | Sell | A protocol fee plus an AMM sell fee (stays in the market's pool) |
    | Trade on X | No transaction fee |
    | Trade in the web app | A small network fee in USDC may be added |
    | Claim winnings or a refund | No protocol fee; the claim's network fee is deducted from the payout |
    | Deposit | No Kash fee; the payment partner may charge its own |
    | Launch a market | Shown in the launch panel; you fund the market's starting liquidity |

    The app shows the exact fees before you confirm.
  </Accordion>


  <Accordion title="How do network fees work?">
    Every Solana transaction has a small network fee in SOL. Kash pays it for you, so you never need SOL. On web trades and claims, a small amount is charged back in USDC, and the app shows it before you confirm. Trades placed on X skip it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Trades

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do trades work?">
    You trade against the market's automated market maker, so there's no counterparty to wait for. Each trade is quoted before you confirm and executes on Solana from your own wallet. See [Custom AMM](/getting-started/features/custom-amm).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel or modify a trade?">
    A trade can't be cancelled once it executes. To change your position, sell some or all of it, or buy more, any time before trading closes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did my trade fail?">
    Common reasons:
    - **The price moved** past your quote before execution (slippage protection stopped the trade)
    - **Trading has closed:** the market froze, resolved or was cancelled
    - **Insufficient USDC** for the amount plus fees
    - **Trading isn't available in your region**

    A failed trade doesn't take your USDC. Refresh the quote and try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Claims and payouts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I claim my winnings?">
    When a market resolves, winning positions appear under **Claimable** in **Portfolio**. Tap **Claim winnings**, or **Claim all**. For cancelled markets, tap **Claim refund**. See [Winning](/getting-started/guides/winning).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much will I receive when I win?">
    Your share of the market's pool: your winning shares ÷ all winning shares × the pool, minus the claim's small network fee. The trade ticket's **estimated payout if you win** is your best guide.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Withdrawals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do withdrawals take?">
    Crypto withdrawals usually complete in one to two minutes. An exchange may take longer to credit you.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I make several withdrawals at once?">
    One withdrawal can be in progress at a time. If one is interrupted, it clears automatically and you can try again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Tracking and problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I track my transactions?">
    **Portfolio → Transactions** lists your trades, claims, deposits and transfers, and **Portfolio → Withdrawals** tracks withdrawals. Each on-chain transaction links to its details, so you can verify it on Solana.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I sent funds to the wrong address?">
    On-chain transfers can't be reversed. If you sent to an exchange on the wrong network, contact the exchange, as they can sometimes recover it. If you sent the wrong token or network to your Kash address, contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) with the transaction details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a transaction problem?">
    Report it from the info button in the app, or email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) with the time, amount and transaction link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
