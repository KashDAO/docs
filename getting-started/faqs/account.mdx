# Account creation and setup

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a Kash account?">
    Go to [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), click **Sign in**, and choose X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord. Your account and your own embedded Solana wallet are set up automatically, with no seed phrase. See [Creating an Account](/getting-started/guides/creating-an-account).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does my wallet ask for trading permission?">
    This permission, given at the **Approve Kash** step, lets Kash's app submit the trades you ask for, in the app, on X or through copy trading, from your own wallet without a signing prompt each time. Without it, trading isn't possible. Your funds stay in your wallet, Kash never holds custody of them, and withdrawals to outside addresses are confirmed by you in the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I link more than one sign-in method?">
    Yes. In **Settings → Account → Recovery methods** you can link X, Google, Discord, Telegram, email and a passkey. Linking more than one means losing one doesn't lock you out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to connect X?">
    Only to create markets, to trade from X, or to announce your markets from your X account. Browsing and trading in the web app work without it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Recovery and security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if I lose access to my login method?">
    Sign in with another linked method, your recovery email or your passkey. If none of those work, contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) from an email address linked to your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I secure my account?">
    - Turn on two-factor authentication with your sign-in provider
    - Add a recovery email or passkey
    - Never share login codes or your exported private key

    See [Security](/getting-started/faqs/security).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my wallet or account data?">
    - **Wallet:** **Settings → Account → Wallet backup** exports your Solana private key, so you can import it into a compatible Solana wallet.
    - **Data:** **Settings → Account → Export my data** downloads your account data as a file.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Account management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I update my profile?">
    Your public title, theme and language are in **Settings**. Your display name comes from your sign-in provider. If you signed up with email only, you can edit it in Settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete my account?">
    Yes. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account** and type `DELETE` to confirm. Your profile is anonymized and your personal data removed, while trading and financial records are kept as required for compliance. Withdraw your balance first. If self-service deletion is unavailable, the page offers an email request to [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get help?">
    Send feedback from the info button in the app (signed-in reports open a support chat in **Messages**), email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot), or ask in [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/n9eumqjMPb). See [Support](/getting-started/faqs/support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
