# Account creation and setup

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a Kash web app account?">
    Go to the Kash web app at [app.kash.bot](https://app.kash.bot), click **Sign in**, and choose X, Google, email, Telegram or Discord. Your account and your own self-custodial embedded Solana wallet are set up automatically, with no seed phrase. See [Creating an Account](/getting-started/guides/creating-an-account).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does my self-custodial wallet ask for trading permission?">
    This permission, given at the **Approve Kash** step, lets the Kash app connect your self-custodial wallet to the Kash protocol so that the trades you want to make –through the app, on X or through copy trading– can be submitted from your own self-custodial wallet without a signing prompt each time. Without it, trading isn't possible. Your funds stay in your self-custodial wallet, ZeroID never holds custody of them, and withdrawals to outside addresses are confirmed by you in the web app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I link more than one sign-in method?">
    Yes. In **Settings → Account → Recovery methods** you can link X, Google, Discord, Telegram, email and a passkey. Linking more than one means losing one doesn't lock you out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to connect X?">
    Only to create markets, to trade from X, or to announce your markets from your X account. Browsing and trading through the web app work without it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Recovery and security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if I lose access to my login method?">
    Sign in with another linked method, your recovery email or your passkey. If none of those work, send feedback from the info button in the web app or ask in [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/n9eumqjMPb). Never share login codes or your private key.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I secure my account?">
    - Turn on two-factor authentication with your sign-in provider
    - Add a recovery email or passkey
    - Never share login codes or your exported private key

    See [Security](/getting-started/faqs/security).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export my self-custodial wallet or account data?">
    - **Wallet:** **Settings → Account → Wallet backup** exports your self-custodial wallet's Solana private key, so you can import it into a compatible Solana wallet.
    - **Data:** **Settings → Account → Export my data** downloads your account data as a file.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Account management

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I update my profile?">
    Your public title, theme and language are in **Settings**. Your display name comes from your sign-in provider. If you signed up with email only, you can edit it in Settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I delete my account?">
    Yes. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete account** and type `DELETE` to confirm. Your profile is anonymized and your personal data removed, while trading and financial records are kept as required for compliance. Withdraw your balance first. If self-service deletion is unavailable, the page offers an email request instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get help?">
    Send feedback from the info button in the web app (signed-in reports open a support chat in **Messages**), or ask in [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/n9eumqjMPb). See [Support](/getting-started/faqs/support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
