# Settings overview

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What settings are available?">
    | Page | What you can do |
    |---|---|
    | **General** | Choose your public title, theme (light, dark or system) and language |
    | **Account** | See your wallet, manage recovery methods, back up your wallet key, set cookie preferences, export your data, sign out or delete your account |
    | **Notifications** | Push notifications, notification types, price alerts and installing the app |
    | **X bot** | Connect your own X account to announce your markets and let followers trade in replies |
    | **Market creation** | Defaults for your market announcements: format, launch and resolution posts, and channels |
    | **API keys** | Create and revoke keys for the Kash API, with scoped permissions and spending controls |
    | **Copy trading** | Manage the traders you copy: size, budget, pause or stop |
    | **Distribution** | Connect your Discord and Telegram channels so your new markets are announced there |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I change the language or theme?">
    In **Settings → General**. Choose from the available languages, and set the theme to light, dark or your system's setting.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I control who can message me?">
    In Settings, choose whether **everyone** or only **mutual followers** can send you messages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I trade from X?">
    Connect your X account (sign in with X, or connect it from **Settings → Account**), then reply to a market's post and mention @kash_bot_trade. No extra setting is needed. See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x). The **X bot** page is a different thing: it posts *your* markets from *your* X account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I manage API keys?">
    In **Settings → API keys**. Each key has scoped permissions, its own spending controls and an optional webhook URL, and you can revoke a key at any time. See the [Developer Docs](/developer-docs/introduction).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What privacy controls do I have?">
    - Accept or reject analytics cookies in **Settings → Account**
    - Choose who can message you
    - Export your data, or delete your account
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Account-level settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I add a recovery method?">
    **Settings → Account → Recovery methods.** Add a recovery email or a passkey, and link or unlink sign-in methods.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I export my wallet key?">
    **Settings → Account → Wallet backup.** Confirm the security checklist, and your Solana private key is shown in a secure window. You can import it into a compatible Solana wallet. Kash never sees your private key. Store it offline and never share it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Troubleshooting settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why aren't my settings saving?">
    Refresh the page and try again, and make sure you're still signed in. If it keeps happening, report it from the info button in the app.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why aren't push notifications working?">
    Check that push is on in **Settings → Notifications**, that your browser allows notifications for app.kash.bot, and on iPhone or iPad that you've added Kash to your home screen and enabled push from the installed app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
