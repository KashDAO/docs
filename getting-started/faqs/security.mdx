# How custody works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who controls my funds?">
    Your USDC and positions sit in **your own embedded Solana wallet**, set up when you sign up. Kash is a non-custodial interface: it never holds custody of user funds and doesn't pool them. Trades and redemptions move USDC directly between your wallet and the on-chain market. You can export your wallet's private key at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can Kash do with my wallet?">
    When you sign in, the **Approve Kash** step asks you to give Kash's app permission to submit transactions from your wallet on your behalf. Kash uses it to submit the trades and claims you request, in the app, on X, through copy trading or with an API key, without a signing prompt each time. Withdrawals to outside addresses are confirmed by you in the app, and transfers to other Kash users are sent only when you send them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a seed phrase? How does recovery work?">
    There's no seed phrase. Access is tied to your sign-in methods, and you can add a recovery email or passkey in **Settings → Account → Recovery methods**. For an independent backup, export your private key from **Settings → Account → Wallet backup**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are markets secured?">
    Markets live on a decentralized, permissionless protocol on Solana that anyone can use directly, so every trade and settlement is recorded on-chain. Outcomes are determined by Kash's automated resolution system from each market's published sources and rules, and go through a dispute window before they settle.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Account security best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I secure my Kash account?">
    - Turn on two-factor authentication with your sign-in provider (X, Google, Telegram, Discord or your email account)
    - Add a recovery email or passkey, and link more than one sign-in method
    - Only sign in at **app.kash.bot**
    - Never share login codes or your exported private key
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?">
    1. Secure the account you sign in with: change its password and review its active sessions
    2. Sign in to Kash and check **Portfolio → Transactions** and **Withdrawals**
    3. If you still control the account, consider moving your funds to a wallet you control
    4. Contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) straight away
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I lose my device?">
    Your wallet isn't stored only on your device. Sign in on a new device with any linked method. Then secure the lost device's accounts (email, X, Google) with your provider.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Common security concerns

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I spot phishing?">
    - The app is only at **app.kash.bot**, and the docs at **docs.kash.bot**
    - Kash will never DM you asking for your private key, a login code or a "verification" deposit
    - Kash has not launched a token. Any "KASH" presale or airdrop is a scam
    - The official X accounts are [@kash_bot](https://x.com/kash_bot) and [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can use Kash?">
    Availability, eligibility and any verification requirements are set out in the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use). See also [Legal](/getting-started/faqs/legal).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Reporting security issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I report a vulnerability?">
    Email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) with **"Security report"** in the subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and its impact. Please don't disclose it publicly until we've had a chance to fix it, test only against your own account, and never against other users' funds or data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
