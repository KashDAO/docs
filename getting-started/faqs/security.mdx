# How custody works

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who controls my funds?">
    Your USDC and positions sit in **your own self-custodial embedded Solana wallet**, set up when you sign up. The Kash web app is a non-custodial interface: ZeroID never holds custody of user funds and doesn't pool them. Trades and redemptions move USDC directly between your self-custodial wallet and the on-chain market. You can export your wallet's private key at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can the Kash web app do with my self-custodial wallet?">
    When you sign in, the **Approve Kash** step asks you to give the Kash app permission to connect your self-custodial wallet to the Kash protocol so that your transactions can be submitted to the protocol, without a signing prompt each time. Withdrawals to outside addresses are confirmed by you in the web app, and transfers to other Kash users are sent only when you send them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a seed phrase? How does recovery work?">
    There's no seed phrase. Access is tied to your sign-in methods, and you can add a recovery email or passkey in **Settings → Account → Recovery methods**. For an independent backup, export your private key from **Settings → Account → Wallet backup**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are markets secured?">
    Markets live on the Kash protocol, a decentralized, permissionless protocol on Solana that anyone can use directly, so every trade and settlement is recorded on-chain. Outcomes are proposed by an automated resolution system operated by ZeroID from each market's published sources and rules, and go through a dispute window before they settle. ZeroID staff decide any dispute. See [Market Mechanics](/getting-started/features/market-mechanics#resolution).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Account security best practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I secure my Kash web app account?">
    - Turn on two-factor authentication with your sign-in provider (X, Google, Telegram, Discord or your email account)
    - Add a recovery email or passkey, and link more than one sign-in method
    - Only sign in at **app.kash.bot**
    - Never share login codes or your exported private key
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access?">
    1. Secure the account you sign in with: change its password and review its active sessions
    2. Sign in to the Kash web app and check **Portfolio → Transactions** and **Withdrawals**
    3. If you still control the account, consider moving your funds to a wallet you control
    4. Contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) straight away
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I lose my device?">
    Your self-custodial wallet isn't stored only on your device. Sign in on a new device with any linked method. Then secure the lost device's accounts (email, X, Google) with your provider.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Common security concerns

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I spot phishing?">
    - The Kash web app is only at **app.kash.bot**, and the docs at **docs.kash.bot**
    - ZeroID will never DM you asking for your private key, a login code or a "verification" deposit
    - Neither ZeroID nor the Kash protocol has launched a token. Any "KASH" presale or airdrop is a scam
    - The official X accounts are [@kash_bot](https://x.com/kash_bot) and [@kash_bot_trade](https://x.com/kash_bot_trade)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who can use the Kash web app?">
    Availability, eligibility and any verification requirements are set out in the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use). See also [Legal](/getting-started/faqs/legal).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Reporting security issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I report a vulnerability?">
    Email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) with **"Security report"** in the subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it and its impact. Please don't disclose it publicly until we've had a chance to fix it, test only against your own account, and never against other users' funds or data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
