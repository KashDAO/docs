# Notifications

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What notifications does Kash send?">
    Kash notifies you about the things that affect your account and markets, including:
    - **Market results:** markets you hold have resolved or been cancelled, and positions or refunds are ready to claim
    - **Trading:** your trades and copy trades
    - **New markets** and **market announcements**
    - **Deposits**, withdrawals and transfers
    - **Price alerts** you've set
    - **Direct messages**
    - **Social and achievements:** new followers, unlocked achievements
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I receive notifications?">
    - **In the app:** the **Notifications** page, with filters by type
    - **Push notifications** to your browser or device

    Kash doesn't send notification emails, and bot replies on X are trade confirmations rather than notifications.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I manage my notification preferences?">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications**. Turn push on or off, and choose which types to receive: market results, new markets, deposits, price alerts, market announcements and direct messages. You can also manage your price alerts there.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get push notifications on my phone?">
    Install Kash as an app from **Settings → Notifications → Install app**. On iPhone and iPad, add Kash to your home screen first, then enable push from the installed app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
