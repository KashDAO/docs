# Common issues and solutions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't access the website">
    - Make sure you're at **app.kash.bot**
    - Refresh, or try a private window to rule out extensions and cached data
    - If you see "Service unavailable in your region", Kash isn't available where you are. See [Legal](/getting-started/faqs/legal)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My trade didn't go through">
    - **Check the market is open.** Frozen, resolved and cancelled markets can't be traded
    - **Check your balance** covers the amount plus fees
    - **Refresh the quote.** If the price moved, slippage protection stops the trade rather than filling it at a worse price
    - A failed trade doesn't take your USDC

    See [Why did my trade fail?](/getting-started/faqs/transactions)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My balance looks incorrect">
    - Refresh the page
    - Check **Portfolio → Transactions** for pending deposits, trades or withdrawals
    - Remember that open positions are valued at current prices, and resolved positions show as claimable until you claim them
    - A deposit sent on the wrong network or as the wrong token won't be credited
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My trade on X didn't work">
    The bot replies with the reason. The usual ones:
    - **Not replying to or quoting the market post:** a mention on its own or a pasted link isn't traded
    - **Wrong account:** mention the account that posted the market
    - **"Sign up first" or "wallet not linked":** create a Kash account and connect your X account
    - **"Approve wallet signing first":** finish the one-time approval in the app
    - **Amount too small**, or not enough USDC
    - **Too late:** the mention was too old by the time it was processed, or the market has closed

    See [Trading on X](/getting-started/features/trading-on-x).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Platform access issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Mobile issues">
    Kash works in mobile browsers, and you can install it from **Settings → Notifications → Install app** (on iPhone and iPad, use **Add to Home Screen**). If something looks broken, update your browser and refresh.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Login problems">
    - Use the same sign-in method you signed up with, or another linked method
    - For email sign-in, check spam for the one-time code and use the newest code
    - If you've lost access to your sign-in method, use your recovery email or passkey. See [Account](/getting-started/faqs/account)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Geographic restrictions">
    Availability, eligibility and any verification requirements are set out in the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use). Trading, deposits and withdrawals are blocked if your account's registration country is restricted or couldn't be confirmed. Using a VPN to get around this breaks the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Technical issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Browser compatibility">
    Use a current version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge, and allow cookies for app.kash.bot so sign-in works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slow performance">
    Close unused tabs, check your connection, and refresh. If the app stays slow, report it from the info button.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Getting help

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I report an issue?">
    Use the info button in the app → **Feedback** → **Report a bug**. Describe what happened and attach a screenshot or short video. If you're signed in, a support chat opens in **Messages** and Kash Support replies there. You can also email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I think my account is compromised">
    Follow the steps in [Security](/getting-started/faqs/security) and contact [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot) straight away.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
