# Platform availability and compliance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Kash available in my country?">
    Kash is not available in sanctioned regions. Users there can't sign up, and trading, deposits and withdrawals are blocked for accounts registered there. The [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use) govern who may use Kash; read them before you trade.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there age requirements?">
    The eligibility requirements, including age, are set out in the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to verify my identity?">
    Kash doesn't ask for identity documents to create an account or trade. The third-party payment provider that runs the deposit checkout may ask you to verify your identity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Tax and financial responsibilities

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are my tax obligations?">
    You're responsible for any taxes on your trading in your jurisdiction. Your full trade, claim and withdrawal history is in **Portfolio**, and you can download your data from **Settings → Account → Export my data**. Kash doesn't provide tax advice; consult a qualified professional.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if regulations change?">
    Kash may change where it's available or how features work to stay compliant. Material changes are reflected in the Terms of Use and announced through official channels.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Community guidelines and privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the community guidelines?">
    Be respectful, don't spam or harass, don't share false information, and don't attempt market manipulation. Display names may not impersonate Kash. Accounts that break the [Terms of Use](https://kash.bot/terms-of-use) can be restricted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What about data privacy?">
    The [Privacy Policy](https://kash.bot/privacy-policy) explains what data Kash collects and why. You can:
    - accept or reject analytics cookies in **Settings → Account**
    - export your data from **Settings → Account → Export my data**
    - delete your account from **Settings → Account → Delete account** (financial records are kept as required by law)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
