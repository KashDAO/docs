# Your wallet

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where are my funds stored?">
    In **your own embedded Solana wallet**, set up when you signed up. You own it, and Kash never holds custody of your funds: trades and redemptions move USDC directly between your wallet and each on-chain market. You can export the wallet's private key from **Settings → Account → Wallet backup**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I check my balance?">
    Your USDC balance is shown in the app header and in **Portfolio**, along with the value of your open positions and anything waiting to be claimed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What currencies does Kash support?">
    **USDC on Solana.** Every price, trade, redemption and withdrawal is in USDC. You never need SOL, because Kash pays the Solana network fees.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Deposits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I deposit funds?">
    Open **Add funds**. Use the checkout to buy or transfer USDC with the payment methods available in your region, or send USDC on Solana straight to your wallet address. See [Depositing](/getting-started/guides/depositing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do deposits take?">
    A USDC transfer on Solana is credited once it confirms on-chain. Checkout purchases depend on the payment method, and the checkout shows the expected time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I deposit other tokens, or USDC on another network?">
    Send only **USDC on Solana** to your wallet address. Other tokens, or USDC sent on a different network, won't be credited and may be unrecoverable. If your funds are elsewhere, use the checkout, which delivers USDC on Solana.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Withdrawals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I withdraw funds?">
    Choose **Withdraw**, enter the amount and the destination address, check the network, and confirm. You can follow its status in the app. See [Withdrawing](/getting-started/guides/withdrawing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send USDC to another Kash user?">
    Yes, from a chat in **Messages**. If you can message someone, you can pay them. Any network fee is shown before you confirm.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there minimum balance requirements?">
    No. There's no minimum balance to keep an account. Some actions have minimums, and the app or the bot tells you if an amount is too small.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Funds from before the move to Solana

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I have USDC from Kash's earlier network. What do I do?">
    Kash markets used to run on Base. If you still hold USDC there, the app shows that balance with two options:
    - **Move to Solana · One-way:** moves it to your Solana wallet so you can trade it on current markets. A bridging fee is shown before you confirm, and the move can't be reversed. The app tells you if an amount can't be moved.
    - **Withdraw** it to an outside address instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

# Wallet security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I keep my wallet secure?">
    Use two-factor authentication with your sign-in provider, add a recovery email or passkey, and never share your exported private key or login codes. See [Security](/getting-started/faqs/security).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do if I have wallet issues?">
    Refresh the app and check **Portfolio → Transactions**. If something still looks wrong, report it from the info button in the app or email [support@kash.bot](mailto:support@kash.bot).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
